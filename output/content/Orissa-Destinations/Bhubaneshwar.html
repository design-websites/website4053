Bhubaneshwar: The Temple City

An ancient capital where glorious history, realistic sculptures, and architectural wonders converge.

City Overview

Ancient Bhubaneshwar, known as the Temple City of India, is the capital and largest city of Odisha. Historically referred to by names such as Toshali, Kalinga Nagari, and Ekamra Khetra, it was established as the political capital in 1948. Along with Cuttack, it is recognized as part of the "twin cities" and is rapidly emerging as a prominent IT hub.

Climate

The city experiences a tropical climate, characterized by dry and typically wet seasons.

Summer: Shoots up to 40°C - 45°C
Winter: Dips to a minimum of 10°C

Average Annual Rainfall: 154 cm

Major Pilgrimage Attractions

Bhubaneshwar completes the religious Golden Triangle of Eastern India alongside Puri and Konark. The temples here are built in the distinct Kalinga style of architecture.

Lingaraj Temple

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this 11th-century masterpiece stands on a 55m-high carved surface. It is a vital pilgrimage stop, traditionally visited before the Jagannath Temple at Puri.

Mukteshwara Temple

A 10th-century gem showcasing the transition of Kalinga architecture. It is famous for its magnificent arched gateway, or torana, and incredibly realistic sculptures.

Dhauli Giri

Located 8km away, this site witnessed the Kalinga War. It houses Ashokan edicts and symbols of Buddhism, marking where Ashoka the Great embraced the path of peace.

Khandagiri & Udayagiri

Twin hills featuring artistic rock-cut caves of an ancient Jain monastery. Notable caves include the Hati Gufa and the two-story Rani Gufa.

Cultural & Institutional Landmarks

Museums

The Odisha State Museum and the Museum of Tribal Art & Artefacts offer deep insights into the region's heritage and tribal traditions.

Nandankanan Zoo

A major attraction for nature lovers, known for its wildlife conservation efforts and diverse animal species.

Unique Sites

Look out for Traffic Mahadev, a small Shiva temple that uniquely serves as a road divider in the city.

Visitor Info

Architecture

Built in the traditional Kalinga style, symbolizing centuries of Oriya culture.

Holy Places

  • Rajarani Temple
  • Ram Mandir
  • ISKCON Temple
  • Brahmeswar Temple

Key Facts

Bhubaneshwar and Cuttack are known as the "twin cities." It is a major economic and religious hub of Eastern India.
