Puri: The Abode of Lord Jagannath

A sacred coastal city where the spiritual meets the sea.

Quick Facts

Area: 2750 sq.km
Altitude: 559.31 meters
Best Time: Nov to March
Rainfall: 1160 mms
Languages: Oriya, Bengali, Hindi, English
Known As: Nilachal, Nilagiri, Niladri

Overview

Puri is one of the four celebrated religious centers of India. One of the oldest cities in the eastern part of the country, it became widely known as Jagannath Puri after the legendary Jagannath Temple was constructed in the 11th century.

Situated on the coast of the Bay of Bengal, Puri is also a popular beach resort. It occupies a unique position where both sunrise and sunset can be viewed from the beach. In Sanskrit, "Puri" means town or city, but the name is deeply intertwined with its presiding deity, Lord Jagannath.

Major Attractions

Temples in Puri

The Jagannath Temple is a premier Hindu pilgrimage destination. It features four gates and a 16-sided monolithic pillar known as 'Arunastambh'. The temple is also home to the largest kitchen in the world, serving 10,000 devotees daily.

Other notable shrines include the Gundicha Mandir, Narasimha temple, and Tota Gopinath temple.

Beaches in Puri

Puri Beach is among the most famous in India. Millions of tourists visit year-round to take a holy bath, believing it to be a golden path to Moksha (eternal salvation).

Fairs and Festivals

Chandana Yatra

Celebrated in April, deities are taken for boat rides in Narendra Sarovara after bathing in sandalwood water.

Snana Yatra

The Bath Festival where 108 pots of water are poured on the deities in a grand procession.

Rath Yatra (Chariot Festival)

Held in June-July, this world-famous festival involves three colossal chariots bearing Jagannath, Balarama, and Subhadra. Lord Jagannath's chariot stands 45 feet high and is supported by 16 wheels. Over 4,000 people drag the chariots to Gundicha Mandir.

Anavasara: A period when Lord Jagannath is said to suffer from fever and is offered medicinal juices.
Nava-Kalevara: A rare ritual when Lord Jagannath receives a new body.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport is Bhubaneswar (60 km), with flights to Delhi, Kolkata, Mumbai, and more.

By Road

Well-connected by a network of roads from all nearby areas and major cities.

By Rail

Connected to major hubs including Bhubaneswar, Kolkata, Varanasi, Hyderabad, and Chennai.
