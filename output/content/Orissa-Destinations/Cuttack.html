Cuttack: The Silver City

A millennium-old aesthetic capital nestled in the delta of the Mahanadi River.

Overview

Cuttack, one of the twin cities of Odisha, was formerly the political and commercial capital of the state. The city is over a millennium old and is established around the ancient Barabati Fort.

Situated at the center of the Mahanadi River delta, the city is embraced by the tributaries Kathjori, Kuakhai, and Birupa. Cuttack is also the heart of the Oriya film industry.

A Tapestry of Faith

Cuttack is home to a diverse array of religious sites for Hindus, Muslims, Christians, and Sikhs.

Hindu Temples

  • Katak Chandi Temple
  • Paramahansa Nath Temple
  • Dhabaleswar Temple (with unique hanging bridge)
  • Panchamukhi Hanuman Temple
  • Gada Chandi Temple
  • Bhattarika Temple

Other Shrines

  • Sikh: Gurdwara Daatan Sahib
  • Muslim: Qadam-E-Rasool, Shahi Mosque, Juma Masjid, Ujale Khan Mosque
  • Christian: Holy Rosary Church, Oriya Baptist Church

Historical Landmarks

Barabati Fort

The ruins of this nine-storied medieval fort, with its moat and gate, stand on the banks of the Mahanadi.

Stone Revetment

An engineering marvel of 11th-century Odisha found along the river banks of Cuttack.

Museums & Parks

The Netaji Museum (Janakinath Bhawan), Freedom Fighters Memorial, Biju-Pattnaik Park, and the Deer Park are essential cultural stops.

Shopping & Crafts

Cuttack is a premier shopping destination in the state, famous for its intricate arts and crafts.

Silver Filigree (Tarakasi)

The oldest and most famed craft in the city, which earned Cuttack its status as the "Silver City".

Other Specialties

Articles made from animal horns, brass work, and traditional silk and cotton textiles.

Visitor Info

Climate

Summer: Touches 40°C
Winter: Dips below 10°C

Sports Hub

Barabati Stadium is a key venue for international cricket. The city also features the Jawaharlal Nehru Indoor Stadium, Satyabrata Stadium, and a SAI training center.

Key Facts

Altitude: 14.62 meters
Significance: Commercial Capital of Odisha
