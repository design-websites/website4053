Aizawl: Home of the Highlanders

A picturesque capital blessed with exotic beauty and a rich tribal heritage.

Quick Facts

Area: 110 sq.km
Altitude: 3500 feet above sea level
Best Time: October to March
Languages: Mizo, English
Summer: 20°C - 30°C
Winter: 11°C - 21°C

An Overview of Aizawl

Aizawl is the largest and capital city of Mizoram, housing more than 26% of the state's population. The name 'Aizawl' translates to "the home of the Highlanders."

The Mizos are predominantly Christian and the state boasts a literacy rate of 87%, the second highest in India. Historically, Mizoram was part of Assam until 1972 (Union Territory) and was declared a full state in 1987.

Places to Visit

The State Museum

An impressive repository of Mizo history and culture, featuring traditional dresses, implements, and historic relics.

Mini Zoo

Set in a picturesque location, this complex houses rare animal species, most notably the endangered Sun Bear.

Tamdil Lake

Located 85 kms from Aizawl, this natural lake is a popular picnic spot and a must-visit for its serene beauty.

Vantawng Falls

At 137 kms from the city, these are the largest and most significant falls in Mizoram, surrounded by lush bamboo groves.

Murlen National Park

Situated 245 kms east of Aizawl near the Indo-Myanmar border, this park is a haven for biodiversity. It accommodates Tigers, Himalayan Bears, Sambar Deer, Barking Deer, Serow, and a vast variety of orchids and birds.

Fairs and Festivals

Chapchar Kut

Feb-March: Celebrates the clearing of jungles for sowing.

Mim Kut

September: Maize harvest festival in honor of departed souls.

Pawl Kut

December: A grand harvest festival celebrated with traditional pomp.

Travel Guide

By Air

Lengpui Airport (45 km away). Regular flights connect to Kolkata.

By Rail

Nearest Railhead is Silchar (Assam), located 180 km from Aizawl.

By Road

Buses ply from Silchar via National Highway No. 44.
