Shri Mata Vaishno Devi

The million-year-old holy shrine where devotion meets the divine peaks of Trikoot.

The Divine Legend

The holy shrine of Shri Mata Vaishno Devi is considered one of the holiest and most popular pilgrimage sites in India. Known as 'Moonh Maangi Muradein Poori Karne Wali Mata', it is believed that no one returns empty-handed from her shadow.

The holy cave is situated at 5,200 ft in the three-peaked mountain Trikoot. It is sanctified as the place where Mata merged her human form with the astral form of the three Supreme Energies. Instead of statues, the Darshan is in the form of three natural rock formations called the Pindies.

The Yatra (Pilgrimage)

The pilgrimage involves a 12 km trek from Katra. The narrow mountain path features many tea and snack stalls along the way to support devotees. The court of Mata remains open round the clock throughout the year.

Managed by the Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Shrine Board (formed in 1986), the site attracts lakhs of devotees from India and abroad.

Offerings & Donations

Traditional Offerings

Red Chunri, Saries, Cholas, ornaments (Silver/Gold), Dry Fruits, and Flowers. Note: Sweets are not allowed, but Coconuts are permitted inside the Cave.

Bhaint Shops

The Shrine Board operates Bhaint shops in Katra that sell Prashad at fair prices, so there is no need to carry them from the base.

Donations are accepted via cash, cheques, or computerized counters and enjoy tax benefits under section 80-G of the Income Tax Act.

Essential Tips for Yatris

Yatra Slip: Obtain a free Yatra slip from the registration counter near Katra bus stand. You cannot proceed beyond the Banganga check post without it.

Transport: Ensure that porters, ponies, and palanquins have a valid registration card and follow pre-approved rate cards.

Accommodation: Rented stays at Katra, Adkuwari, Sanjichhat, or Bhawan can be booked at the Niharika Complex near the bus stand.

Queue Management: Visit the Yatra slip check post to receive a group number for the Darshan queue.

Final Darshan: Tradition dictates that Darshans at the Bhairon temple are performed only after visiting the Holy Cave.

Trip Planner

Base Camp

Katra, Jammu and Kashmir.

Trek Distance

Approximately 12 km from Katra to the Bhawan.

Preparation

Carry bare minimum belongings. Bathe and wear clean clothes before proceeding for Darshan.
