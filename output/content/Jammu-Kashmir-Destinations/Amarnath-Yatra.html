Amarnath Yatra

Journey to the 14,000 ft high holy shrine of Lord Shiva in the Lidder Valley.

Divine Mythology

Amarnath is a world-famous Hindu shrine located in the Lidder Valley, devoted to Lord Shiva. According to legend, it was in this cave that Shiva revealed the secrets of the creation of the Universe and immortality to Goddess Parvati.

A pair of doves secretly overheard this conversation and became immortal, making the cave their eternal home. Today, lakhs of pilgrims visit annually to witness the naturally formed ice-stalagmite Shivalinga, which grows and wanes with the moon.

The Pilgrimage (Yatra)

Schedule & Timing

The Jammu & Kashmir Government organizes the annual yatra during July and August (the Hindu month of Shravan). Darshan is permitted for one full month, typically from Ashard Purnimashi to Shravan Purnimashi.

Registration Information

Registration usually begins one month prior to the Yatra. Depending on your location, you can register at various centers across India:

New Delhi (Delhi, UP, Haryana)

Kanishka Shopping Complex, 19-Ashok Road.

Mumbai (Maharashtra, MP, Goa)

World Trade Centre, Cuffe Parade, Colaba.

Kolkata (WB, Orissa, Bihar, NE)

12 Chowringhee, Kolkata.

Chennai (TN, Pondicherry)

36/36-A North Uma Road.

Routes & Logistics

Trekking Routes

The traditional 42 km (26 mi) rocky path starts from Pahalgam and takes 4-5 days on foot. A shorter alternate route is available from Baltal. Ponies are available for children and the elderly.

Food & Water

Govt. depots at Chandanwari, Sheshnag, and Panchtami provide essentials at fair prices. While tea stalls line the path, pilgrims are advised to carry biscuits and tinned food.

Medical Assistance

Free medical posts are established throughout the route to assist devotees during the arduous journey.

Yatra Checklist

Registration

Apply at least one month in advance through designated Govt. counters.

Altitude Awareness

The shrine is located at 14,000 ft. Ensure you are physically fit for high-altitude trekking.

Essential Items

Carry high-energy snacks like toffees and biscuits along with warm clothing.
