Dachigam National Park

A stunning alpine sanctuary and the last refuge of the Kashmir Stag.

Park Snapshot

Location: 22 km from Srinagar
Area: 141 sq. km.
Altitude: 1700m - 4300m
Climate: Winter Min 10°C / Summer Max 32°C

History & Legacy

Dachigam National Park was originally established as a wildlife sanctuary in 1910 under the management of the Maharaja of Jammu & Kashmir. It was later upgraded to National Park status in 1981.

Due to its wide variation in altitude (5,500 ft to 14,000 ft), the park is divided into two distinct regions:

  • Upper Dachigam: Best visited from May to August due to harsh winters.
  • Lower Dachigam: Accessible year-round, though September to December is the peak time.

Flora & Landscapes

Situated in the Western Himalayas, the park features a diverse landscape of sloping grasslands, rocky cliffs, and coniferous forests. The mountainsides are dotted with alpine pastures, waterfalls, and surging meadows that bloom with flowers most of the year.

Marsar Lake

A beautiful high-altitude lake running through the sanctuary, perfect for trout fishing.

Forest Variety

Home to Oak, Apricot, Walnut, Chinar, Pine, Wild Cherry, and many more tree species.

Wildlife & Birdlife

The Kashmir Stag (Hangul)

Dachigam is the last proper habitat of the Hangul. Other animals include Leopard, Musk Deer, Himalayan Black Bear, and Himalayan Marmot.

Avian Diversity

Home to 150 bird species including the Golden Eagle, Paradise Flycatcher, and Bearded Vulture. Best bird-watching: March to May.

How to Reach & Important Tips

By Air

Srinagar Airport is the nearest air link, located just 22 km away.

By Rail

Jammu Tawi is the nearest railway station, approximately 310 km from the park.

Mandatory Permit

A special entry permit from the Chief Wildlife Warden is required before entering the park.

Essential Tips

Timings

5:30 AM to 6:30 PM daily.

Entry Fee

No entry fee is required (only the mandatory permit).

Clothing

Carry heavy woolen clothing as the climate can get extremely cold.
