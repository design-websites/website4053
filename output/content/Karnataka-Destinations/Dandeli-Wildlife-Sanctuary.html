Dandeli Wildlife Sanctuary

Home of the mysterious Black Panther and untamed river adventures.

Quick Facts

Location: Uttara Kannada, Karnataka
Area: 5729.07 sq. km
Best Time: October to March
Timings: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Highlights: Black Panther, Rafting, Trekking

Overview

Dandeli Wildlife Sanctuary is the second largest sanctuary in Karnataka, covering a vast expanse of moist deciduous and semi-evergreen forests. It is most famous for being the home of the elusive Black Panther, immortalized as Bagheera in The Jungle Book.

The Mysterious Panther

While sighting these melanistic leopards depends on luck, official records indicate there are eight of them within the sanctuary. Their dark coloration provides a significant hunting advantage in the whispering forests.

The River Kali and its tributaries, the Kaneri and Nagajhar, meander through the park, offering a lifeline to diverse wildlife and providing a playground for adventure seekers.

Wildlife & Adventure

The Inhabitants

Beyond the panther, discover tigers, elephants, gaur (bison), sloth bears, and crocodiles. The forest is also home to wild dogs, jackals, giant flying squirrels, and various species of deer including barking, spotted, and mouse deer.

Birdwatcher's Paradise

The sanctuary is a haven for the Hornbill, found here in large numbers. Other species include the Malabar pied hornbill, crested serpent eagle, magpie robin, and the white-breasted kingfisher.

Must-Visit Sightseeing

Kavala Caves (30 km)

Ancient limestone caves once home to prehistoric man. They feature unique Shivling-like stalagmites and require a trek to reach.

Shiroli Peak (25 km)

The highest point in the district, providing breathtaking views of the Western Ghats and excellent trekking opportunities.

Syntheri Rocks

An imposing 300-ft limestone cliff-face outside the sanctuary, featuring hundreds of beehives and mysterious caves at its base.

Kulgi Nature Camp (14 km)

Features a nature interpretation centre and museum. The highlight is the stuffed remains of a 13-ft long king cobra.

Travel Guide

Activities

  • White Water Rafting (Kali River)
  • Jeep Safaris & Nature Walks
  • Crocodile Sighting Trips
  • Coracle Boat Rides

By Air

Nearest airport is Dabolim (Goa) at a distance of 145 km.

By Rail

Nearest railhead is Londa (25 km). Others include Dharwad (57 km) and Hubli (72 km).

By Road

Dandeli is 120 km from Karwar and well-connected via state highways.
