Bangalore: The Garden City

India's Silicon Valley, known for its pleasant climate, lush parks, and heritage monuments.

Quick Facts

Area: 2190 sq. km
Altitude: 920 m - 949 m above sea level
Best Time: September to February
Languages: Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, English
Summer: 21.2°C - 33.4°C
STD Code: 080

Overview & History

Bangalore, recently rechristened as 'Bengaluru', is the capital of Karnataka and one of the fastest-developing cities in India. Legend says the name originated from "Bendha KaaLu" (boiled beans) after an old woman fed a starving King Veera Ballala. While the legend is popular, 9th-century temple inscriptions in Begur provide historical evidence of the name BengaLooru existing much earlier.

Founded in the 16th century by Kempe Gowda, a chieftain of the Vijayanagar Empire, the city is draped over the Deccan Plateau. Its high altitude gives it possibly the best climate among all Indian cities, earning it the title 'Garden City of India'.

Sightseeing In Bangalore

Bangalore Palace

Constructed in 1887, this architectural wonder is built on the lines of Windsor Castle. It features fortified structures and interiors ornate with elegant wooden carvings.

Shimmering Lakes

The city is home to several recreational hangout spots like Ulsoor Lake, Sankey Tank, and Yediyur Lake, providing a calm escape from the urban hustle.

Art & Heritage

Explore the Venkatappa Art Gallery, featuring works by M.F. Hussain and R. Venkatappa, or the Karnataka Chitrakala Parishat, which houses a rich collection of traditional artifacts.

Amusement & Speed

For thrill-seekers, the Kingfisher Speedzone in Whitefield offers Go-karting, while Neeladri Amusement and Water Park features over 40 fun-filled rides across 30 acres.

Popular Excursions

Devarayanadurga (70 km)

Lush greenery at 1188m altitude. Perfect for adventure activities, visiting ancient twin temples, and a natural spring.

Shivaganga (Height: 1368m)

A remarkable hill that looks like a bull, Ganesha, Shivalinga, or a cobra depending on the direction of view. Offers breathtaking vistas.

Bhimeshwari (100 km)

A tiny village ideal for angling and fishing. Known for its abundant flora, fauna, and riverside resorts.

How to Reach

By Air

Bangalore Airport (6 km from center) handles domestic and international flights from London, Paris, Singapore, and major Indian cities.

By Rail

Major stations include Bangalore City and Bangalore Cantt. The Karnataka Exp. provides daily connectivity to New Delhi.

By Road

Connected to Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra via well-built state and national highways.

Shopping

Head to MG Road, Brigade Road, or Commercial Street for local silk, sandalwood items, and jewellery.
