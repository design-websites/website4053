Mangalore: Coastal Gateway

A vibrant port city of ancient temples, historic churches, and sun-kissed beaches.

City Overview

Mangalore is a vital commercial and trade hub located on the sea coast of the Dakshina Kannada district in Tulu Nadu, Karnataka. Formed as a district hub in August 1997, it serves as one of the five taluks of D.K.

The city offers a unique blend for travelers, featuring a multitude of historic temples and churches for the religious, and scenic beaches for those seeking relaxation and adventure.

Spiritual & Architectural Wonders

Kadri Manjunath Temple

Built in the 14th century on Kadri hills, this temple is dedicated to Manjunatheshwara (an incarnation of Shiva). It houses the oldest installed idol of Lord Manjunathaswamy among South Indian temples. During the Hindu month of Karthika, it is lit with thousands of earthen lamps for Deepotsava.

St. Aloysius College Chapel

A hilltop jewel built to resemble Rome's Sistine Chapel. The interior is a breathtaking masterpiece, featuring a series of intricate paintings on the roof and walls by the renowned artist Bro. Moscheni.

Shri Sharavu Mahaganapathi Temple

A pious pilgrim site in the heart of the city. Originally dedicated to a Shivalinga, it now also houses images of "Sri Dhashabhuja Mahaganapathy" and "Siddilaxmi," serving as a hub for cultural activities.

St. Aloysius Church

Built in the 18th century, the walls of this beautiful church are decorated with stunning paintings by Italian artist Antony Moshaini.

Landmarks & Parks

Sultan Battery

A black stone fortress built by Tipu Sultan in Boloor to protect the Gurpur River from warships. Today, the desolate fortress with its encircling cannons and "Tipu's Well" is a popular tourist destination.

Kadri Park

A stunning garden ideal for families, featuring a toy train and a zoo with snakes and other animals to excite children.

Light House Hill-Garden

Offers spectacular views of the sea, sunset, and sailing ships from a well-maintained hilltop garden.

Adventure & Coastal Life

Mangalore is a prime destination for adventure seekers and beach lovers.

Pilikula Nisarga Dham

A comprehensive recreational park featuring a water theme park ("Manasa"), a golf course, science centre, arboretum, and boating facilities.
