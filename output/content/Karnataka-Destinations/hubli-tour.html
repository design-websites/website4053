Hubli: The Flower Creeper

North Karnataka's industrial powerhouse and major trading center for cotton.

Commercial Hub of North Karnataka

The name Hubli is derived from a Kannada word meaning "Flower creeper." Formerly known as Raya Hubli or Elaya Puravada Halli, the city has evolved into a massive business hub known for its handloom textile units and industrial output.

As the headquarters for the South Western Railway Zone, it houses one of the oldest railway wagon workshops. It is a world-class trading center for groundnuts and cotton, boasting one of the largest cotton markets in India.

Must-Visit Attractions

Chandramouleshwara Temple

Built during the Chalukya era, this ancient temple features a beautiful idol of Lord Shiva in his form as Chandramouleshwara.

Unkal Lake

A popular picnic spot featuring lush green gardens, boating facilities, and recreational areas for children. It offers a spectacular view of the sunset.

Siddharoodha Math

An eminent religious institution and center for Advaita philosophy as preached by the celebrated Swami Siddharoodha.

The Glass House

Inaugurated by the former Governor of Karnataka, this structure resembles the famous glass house in Bangalore's Lalbagh.

Spiritual Diversity

Sayed Fateh Shah Vali Dargah

A symbol of communal harmony where both Hindus and Muslims come to worship. The premises also house educational institutions for girls.

Asar

Originally built as a hall of justice, this site now stores two holy hairs from the Prophet's beard. It is the center of annual Id Milad un Nabi celebrations (Note: Women are not allowed inside).

Bhavanishankar Temple

Another Chalukya masterpiece, this temple depicts the ten incarnations of God surrounding the central image of Sri Narayana.

Nature & Excursions

  • Nripatunga Betta: A favorite picnic spot located on top of Unkal Hill, offering panoramic city views.
  • Sathodi & Mathodi Falls: Nature-gifted waterfalls that are ideal for trekking and photography.
  • Sykes Point: A magnificent viewpoint to enjoy the sunset and the lush greenery of the region.
  • Ulavia: A holy site housing the Shri Chennabasaveshwar temple, serving as a gateway to Jog Falls and Om Beach.

Quick Travel Guide

Economy

Major industries: Steel furniture, food products, rubber, electrical, and leather tanning.

Local Flavor

Don't leave without trying the world-famous Hubli Pedas (sweets).

Connectivity

Hubli is a major railway junction for the South Western zone with excellent rail and road links.

Key Info

Language: Kannada
Significance: Major Cotton Trading Centre
