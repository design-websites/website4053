Mysore: City of Palaces

A land of royal heritage, vibrant gardens, and rich cultural legacy.

Overview

Mysore, whose name is derived from the demon king Mahishasura, is one of the premier tourist destinations in Karnataka. Once the state capital, it has evolved into a modern city that carefully preserves its rich historical and cultural legacy.

Known globally as the "City of Palaces," Mysore is home to numerous royal buildings constructed by former emperors, alongside breathtaking waterfalls, ancient temples, world-class museums, and beautiful gardens.

Must-Visit Attractions

KRS Dam (Kannambadi)

An engineering marvel built by Sir M. Vishveswariah and financed by Maharaja Sri Krishnaraja Wodeyar. It was the first dam in the world to feature automatic sluice gates, forming one of India's largest lakes.

Brindavan Gardens

Designed by Diwan Sir Mirza Ismail, these gardens are famous for their musical dancing fountains. At sunset, thousands of lit fountains dance to pleasant music, creating a magical atmosphere.

Chamundi Hills

Rising 1065m above sea level, the hilltop features the Chamundeshwari Temple. Visitors can climb 1000 granite steps, passing a massive monolith Nandi bull and a gigantic statue of Mahishasura.

Mysore Zoo

One of the oldest zoos in India, flourished under royal patronage. Today, it is a pioneer in captive breeding and features modern facilities for animal welfare.

Heritage & Scenic Spots

St. Philomena’s Church

A majestic structure with 175-foot high spires. It preserves a 3rd-century relic of St. Philomena in a catacomb beneath the main altar.

Lakes of Mysore

The city boasts three beautiful lakes: Karanji Lake (home to India's largest walkthrough aviary), Kukkaranahalli Lake, and Lingabudi Lake. These are prime spots for bird watching and picnics.

Travel Guide

Best Time to Visit

September to February is ideal for exploring the city's gardens and hills.

Photography Tips

Don't miss the evening fountain show at Brindavan Gardens and the panoramic city view from Sykes Point.

Did You Know?

The KRS Dam provides vital water to agricultural lands in Mysore and Mandya districts and generates power for Shivanasamudra.
