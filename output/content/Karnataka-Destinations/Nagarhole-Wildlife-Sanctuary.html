Nagarhole Wildlife Sanctuary

Discover nature in its most refined state along the banks of the Kabini River.

Quick Facts

Area: 644 sq Km
Best Time: September to May
Languages: English, Hindi, Kannada
Climate: Summer (Max 33°C), Winter (Min 14°C)

History & Landscape

Formerly a private hunting ground for the rulers of Mysore, Nagarhole was declared a protected area in the mid-1950s. Today, it offers an immersive experience into nature's refined state, with a terrain featuring lush forests, thick grasslands, and numerous water bodies.

The River Kabini serves as the park's southern boundary, separating it from Bandipur National Park. The riverbanks are a hub of activity where animals assemble to quench their thirst and bask in the sun.

Wildlife & Avifauna

Mammals

  • Major Attraction: Giant Asian Elephant
  • Predators: Tiger, Panther, Wild Dog
  • Others: Bison, Sloth Bear, Spotted Deer, Sambhar, Bonnet Macaque

Birds

Over 250 rare species of birds reside here:

  • Crested Serpent
  • Woodpecker
  • Golden Back Parakeet
  • Crested Hawk
  • Alexandrine
  • Bulbul

Experience the Wilderness

The Forest Department offers mini bus safaris, which are the best way to spot wildlife. While private jeeps are permitted, they must be accompanied by an official guide.

Elephant joy rides are another major attraction for tourists visiting the sanctuary.

Travel Guide

By Air

Bangalore is the nearest airport (235 Km), connected via daily flights to major cities.

By Rail

Mysore (95 Km) is the nearest railhead, with regular trains from Delhi and other hubs.

By Road

State buses and taxis connect Nagarhole to Mysore and Bangalore. Bus fare from Mysore is approx. Rs. 50.
