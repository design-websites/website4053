Jalandhar

The Sports City of India, where ancient legends meet world-class industry.

Sports City of India

Jalandhar is widely known as the 'Sports City of India'. It boasts the country's finest sports industry and has produced a remarkable number of sportsmen. Notably, the village of Sansarpur alone has given India 12 of its greatest hockey players.

The city is a global hub for the export of quality sports goods. The name Jalandhar is either attributed to a demon king mentioned in the Puranas or derived from the vernacular term meaning "area inside the water," referring to the land between the Sutlej and Beas rivers.

Places to Visit

Tulsi Mandir

An ancient monument located in the Kot Kishan Chand locality. It is dedicated to Vrinda, the wife of Jalandhara. Nearby is the Sheetla Mandir, believed to be as old as the city itself.

Shaheed-E-Azam Sardar Bhagat Singh Museum

Located at Khatkal Kalan, this museum honors revolutionary martyrs. It preserves memorable belongings, including the ashes of Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, and Sukhdev.

Gurdwara Chhevin Padshahi

Situated in Basti Shaikh, this Gurdwara marks the spot where Guru Hargobind Ji granted an interview to the Muslim saint, Shaikh Darvesh, during his tour of the Doaba area.

Devi Talab Mandir

A prominent religious site in the city. Other nearby historical monuments include the 800-year-old Imam Nasir Mosque and the two-century-old Jama Masjid.

Historical & Religious Landmarks

Shiv Mandir

Located at Gur Mandi, this temple was built by a Nawab of Sultanpur Lodhi near the Masjid Imam Nasir, showcasing the city's diverse cultural history.

Ancient Mosques

The heart of Jalandhar houses the Imam Nasir Mosque (800 years old) and the historic Jama Masjid, reflecting centuries of architectural heritage.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest Airport is RajaSansi Airport, Amritsar, located approximately 90 km from the city.

By Road

Well-connected via the Delhi-Amritsar Highway. Distance from Chandigarh: 146 km; from Delhi: 350 km.

Surrounding Areas

Bordered by Ludhiana (East), Kapurthala (West), Hoshiarpur (North), and Ferozepur (South).
