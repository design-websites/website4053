Ludhiana

The Lancaster of India: A hub of industry, history, and spiritual heritage.

City Profile

Established in 1480 and named after the Lodhi dynasty, Ludhiana is today one of the most densely populated cities in Punjab. It is world-famous for its textile and light engineering industries.

The River Sutlej serves as a natural boundary separating Ludhiana from Jalandhar. The city is also a major educational and agricultural hub, housing the renowned Punjab Agricultural University, famous for its annual Kisaan Mela.

Historical Monuments

Fort of Lodhi

Built by Sikander Lodhi approximately 500 years ago, this historical monument stands majestically on the banks of the River Sutlej.

Phillaur Fort

Designed by Dewan Mohkam Chand, this ancient fort now serves as a significant police training center.

Religious Significance

Gurudwara Shrimanji Sahib Alamgir

Located 10km from the city, this is where Guru Gobind Singh converted the pacifist Sikh clan into a martial community (The Khalsa). An annual fair is held here in December.

Pir-I-Dastgir Shrine

A famous shrine of Peer Abdul Kadar Gilani in north-west Ludhiana that serves as a unique meeting point for both Hindu and Muslim devotees.

Other notable sites include Gurudwara Charan Kamal, Gurudwara Nanaksar Jagraon, the Bilwanwali Masjid, and the historic tombs of Hussain Khan and Bahadur Khan.

Relaxation & Wildlife

Nehru Rose Garden

Spread over 30 acres, it features 17,000 plants and 1,600 varieties of roses along with beautiful fountains. It hosts a famous Rose Festival annually.

Tiger Safari (Tiger Zoo)

Located 6 km from the city on GT Road, this safari offers a chance to see varieties of majestic tigers in a naturalistic setting.

Modern Highlights

Harry's World

A top attraction for all ages, boasting the third biggest indoor snow world in the country, featuring ice-skating and snowfall.

Museums

Explore the rich local heritage at the Rural Museum and pay tribute at the city's War Museum.

At a Glance

Industry

World-famous for textiles and light engineering.

Key Events

Kisaan Mela, Rose Festival, and the December Fair at Alamgir.

Education

Home to Punjab Agricultural University.
