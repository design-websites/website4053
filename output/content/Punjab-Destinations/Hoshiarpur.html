Hoshiarpur

A historic gateway where the plains of Punjab meet the Shivalik foothills.

Regional Profile

Hoshiarpur shares a common boundary with the Kangra and Una districts of Himachal Pradesh to the northeast. Due to its abundant hilly terrain and forest cover, the district enjoys a milder climate compared to other regions in Punjab.

This climate is particularly conducive for citrus fruits, which serve as the primary agricultural produce of the area.

Ancient Roots & Archaeology

The history of Hoshiarpur stretches back to the Indus Valley Civilization. Excavations at Stone Age sites like Atbarapur, Rehmanpur, and Takhni in the Shivalik foothills have revealed significant stone artifacts.

Epic Connections

Local legends associate several sites with the Pandavas. Dasua is mentioned in the Mahabharata as the seat of Raja Virata, where the Pandavas stayed during their exile.

Dholbaha Remains

Archaeological remains of temples at Dholbaha provide valuable insights into the ancient history and cultural heritage of the district.

Major Attractions

Harike Wetland (Hari-ke-Pattan)

Located approximately 60 km from Hoshiarpur, Harike is one of the largest freshwater wetlands in Northern India. The wetland offers stunning views, particularly from the Gurdwara situated on the bank of Harike Lake.

Hoshiarpur Fun Park

A perfect destination for family leisure, the Fun Park offers a fusion of amusement rides, an open-air theatre, landscaped gardens, and multi-cuisine food outlets.

Nearby Heritage Stops

A visit to Hoshiarpur provides easy access to some of Punjab's most iconic landmarks:

Wagah Border

Experience the colorful Beating the Retreat Ceremony at the India-Pakistan border.

Jallianwala Bagh

A befitting memorial dedicated to the martyrs of the 1919 massacre.
