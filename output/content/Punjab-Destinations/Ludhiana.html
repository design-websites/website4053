Ludhiana

An industrial titan of Punjab rich in history, culture, and nature.

Industrial Heritage

Located on the south bank of River Sutlej, Ludhiana is an important industrial town of Punjab. It serves as a crossroads of modernity and antiquity, featuring both historical monuments and deep-rooted religious centers.

From the world-renowned Punjab Agricultural University to the scenic gardens and majestic forts, Ludhiana offers a diverse palette of experiences for travelers from across the globe.

Major Attractions

Nehru Rose Garden

Spanning 30 acres, this garden is home to over 17,000 plants and 1,600 varieties of roses. It hosts an annual Rose Festival that attracts tourists far and wide.

Tiger Zoo

Experience a tiger safari where majestic tigers roam freely in protected jungles. It offers an exciting sight for families and nature enthusiasts.

Hardy's World

India's 3rd largest indoor snow city. This amusement park offers theme parties, joy rides, water sports, and real snowfall experiences.

Lodhi Fort

A 500-year-old historic fort constructed by Sikander Lodhi during his rule over the plains near the Sutlej River.

Culture & Museums

PAU Museum

The Punjab Agricultural University houses a world-famous museum that poignantly depicts the rural life and culture of Punjabis. It is also home to the annual Kisan Mela.

Maharaja Ranjit Singh War Museum

An international standard tribute to gallant soldiers, featuring galleries for the Kargil War, History, Uniforms, and an Audio-Visual Theatre with light and sound shows.

Sacred Shrines

Gurdwara Charan Kanwal Sahib

Located in Machhiwara, this marks the site where Guru Gobind Singh rested during his guerrilla war against Mughal forces.

Gurudwara Alamgir

Commemorates the spot where Muslim devotees Nabi Khan and Ghani Khan carried Guru Gobind Singh to safety. Hosts a major fair in December.

Bilwanwali Masjid

A Mughal-era mosque from the time of Emperor Jahangir, known for its intricate brick carvings and double-storied decorated gates.

Pir-I-Dastir Shrine

A fort shrine located to the north-west that draws both Hindu and Muslim pilgrims in a unique display of unity.

Travel Snapshot

Economy

Major industrial hub of Punjab, leading in textiles and manufacturing.

Sports

Guru Nanak Stadium features international-standard tracks and hosts National Football League matches.

Major Events

Rose Festival, Kisan Mela, and the December fair at Gurudwara Alamgir.
