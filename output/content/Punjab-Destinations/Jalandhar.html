Jalandhar

The oldest city of Punjab, blending ancient antiquity with modern industrial brilliance.

City Overview

Situated in the heart of Punjab, the Jalandhar District is of undoubted antiquity and is believed to be the oldest city in the state.

Today, Jalandhar stands as a highly industrialized center, serving as India's foremost producer of world-class sports equipment. It offers a unique blend of historical landmarks, religious shrines, and modern amusement centers.

Key Attractions

Devi Talab Mandir

A renovated temple complex featuring a gilded Mandir in the center of a lake. It is world-renowned for the 'Hariballabh Sangeet Sammelan' music festival held annually in December.

Pushpa Gujral Science City

The largest and second Science City in India. It is designed to combine fun and learning for visitors of all ages through interactive science exhibits.

Wonderland Theme Park

An 11-acre amusement and water park offering thrilling rides and water games, perfect for family entertainment and all age groups.

St Mary's Cathedral Church

Located in Jalandhar Cantonment, this cathedral features a rare 'cosmo-culture' design and is the only one of its kind in the East.

Religious & Historical Legacy

Imam Nasir Mausoleum

An 800-year-old beautifully designed mausoleum located in the heart of the city, alongside a 400-year-old Jamma Masjid.

Shiv Mandhir & Tulsi Mandir

Shiv Mandhir dates back to the Lodhi Era and features a unique blend of Muslim-Hindu architecture. Tulsi Mandir, formerly the Temple of Vrinda, is an ancient city monument.

Desh-Bhagat Memorial Hall

A memorial hall constructed to honor the martyrs who sacrificed their lives during the Indian freedom struggle against British rule.

Fairs & Festivals

Hariballabh Sangeet Sammelan

A prestigious musical gathering held at Devi Talab Mandir every December for over 125 years.

Sodal Mandir Fair

Devotees gather in thousands during 'Anand Chaturdashi' in September to seek blessings from the child deity.
