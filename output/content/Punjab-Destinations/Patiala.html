Patiala

A spectrum of Rajput, Mughal, and Punjabi cultures blended into one royal vision.

The Land of Baba Ala Singh

Patiala, meaning the "Pati" (land) of its founder Baba Ala Singh, is a vibrant city in Punjab that synthesizes tradition with modernity. It is globally famous for its distinct cultural markers:

Patiala Turban
Patiala Peg
Traditional Jutti
Paranda & Salwaar

Royal Palaces & Forts

Qila Mubarak

Standing on 10 acres in the heart of the city, this complex is a synthesis of late Mughal and Rajasthani architecture. It contains the main palace (Qila Androon), the guesthouse (Ran Baas), and the grand Darbar Hall.

The surrounding bazaar is famous for precious ornaments, hand-woven fabrics, and colorful footwear.

Sheesh Mahal and Museum

Built under the patronage of Maharaja Narendra Singh, a lover of fine arts. The museum features stunning paintings in the Rajasthani style, depicting mythology, legends, and the Raga-Ragni series.

Moti Bagh Palace

This palace features beautiful Rajasthan-style jharokas and chhatris. It is set within magnificent gardens complete with terraces and water channels.

Spiritual Heritage

Gurudwara Dukhniwaran Sahib

Built on land donated by the villagers of Lehal, this elevated site commemorates the visit of Guru Teg Bahadur. Local legend states that anyone who prays here is relieved of their suffering (Dukhniwaran).

How to Reach

By Air

Chandigarh Airport (IXC): 54 km from the city center.

Sahnewal Airport (LUH): 73 km from Patiala.

By Rail

Patiala Station is on the Ambala-Bathinda line. Nearest main railheads are Ambala Cantonment (60 km) and Rajpura Junction.

By Road

Located just off National Highway 1. Approximately 250 km from Delhi.
