Patiala

A city of magnificent palaces, vibrant culture, and timeless spiritual grace.

Cultural Tradition

Patiala boasts a well-marked cultural tradition. While the city itself is not historically very old, it is home to some of the most impressive architectural and cultural landmarks in Punjab, reflecting a rich heritage of royalty and devotion.

Royal Palaces & Forts

Moti Bagh Palace

Built by Maharaja Narinder Singh, this grand palace now encompasses the National Institute for Sports, making it a favorite for sports enthusiasts.

Sheesh Mahal

The "Palace of Mirrors" served as a pleasure complex. It features chambers with intricate mirror work and paintings in the Kangra and Rajasthani qalam styles, along with a museum and art gallery.

Quila Mubarak

The historic heart of the city, consisting of the inner Quila Androon and the outer Quila Mubarak. It features painted chambers depicting Hindu mythology and Sikh Gurus.

Diwan Khana

A grand hall for public occasions, now a museum displaying dazzling chandeliers and historical armor, including the sword and dagger of Guru Gobind Singh.

Spiritual Heritage

Gurudwara Dukh Nivaran Sahib

Built on a site visited by Guru Teg Bahadur. Legend holds that anyone who prays here with devotion is relieved of their suffering (Dukhniwaran).

Kali Temple

Built by Maharaja Bhupinder Singh, this temple houses a 6-ft statue of Goddess Kali brought from Bengal and attracts devotees from all faiths.

Bahadurgarh Fort

A massive fort with circular ramparts and a wide moat, built to commemorate the visit of Guru Teg Bahadur.

Gardens & Public Spaces

Baradari Gardens

Surrounded by stone porticos (baradaries), these gardens feature rare trees, colonial buildings, and the historic Fern House.

Mall Road

The vibrant heart of the city's social life, featuring fountains, beautiful paved walkways, and various entertainment spots.

Highlights

Sports Hub

Home to the prestigious National Institute for Sports at Moti Bagh Palace.

Art Preservation

The North Zone Cultural Centre in Banasar Ghar works to preserve regional arts.

Lakshman Jhoola

A suspension bridge over the lake connecting Sheesh Mahal to Banasar Ghar.
