Gir National Park

The last refuge of the Asiatic Lion in the heart of Gujarat's dry deciduous forest.

Sanctuary Profile

Area: 1412 Sq. Km
Altitude: 40m to 50m
Best Time: Mid October to Mid June
Temperature: 12°C to 40°C

Pro Tip: November and February are ideal for lion sightings.

Conservation History

The history of Gir is defined by the preservation of Asiatic lions, whose population dwindled to just 15 individuals in the early 20th century due to trophy hunting.

Alarmed by this decline, British viceroys and specifically Lord Curzon requested the Nawab of Junagadh to intervene. The Nawab declared the forest and its lions protected and imposed a strict ban on hunting.

Today, Gir stands as the largest compact tract of dry deciduous forest in western India, supporting the highest concentration of carnivores and the largest population of marsh crocodiles in the country.

Beyond the Wild

Jeep Safari

Observation of the big cats is best at dawn and dusk. Driving through the forest in a jeep provides the most intimate view of lions in their natural surroundings.

Junagadh Heritage

Explore the virtually inaccessible Uparkot Fort, the Maqbara mausoleums of the Nawabs, and the ancient fourteen rock edicts of Emperor Ashoka.

Nearby Points of Interest

  • Beaches: Visit Chorwad, a sunny coastal resort, or Ahmadpur Mandvi, noted as one of India's finest beaches.

  • Temples: Somnath Temple is one of India's twelve most sacred Shiva shrines. The Damodar sacred tank and Girnar temples are also essential stops.

  • Tribal Culture: Engage with local tribes who still reside inside the park, co-existing with wildlife and maintaining unique lifestyles.

Visitor Guidelines

» Take official guides for every trip.

» Do not tease or irritate animals.

» Smoking is strictly prohibited.

» No flash photography or intrusive gear.

» Camping and picnicking are not allowed.

» Wear simple clothes that blend with nature.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport: Keshod (90 km away). Daily flights available from Mumbai.

By Rail

Nearest connection: Meter gauge line from Ahmedabad (395 km).

By Road

Situated 400 km from Ahmedabad. State buses run from Junagadh and Veraval between Nov and June.
