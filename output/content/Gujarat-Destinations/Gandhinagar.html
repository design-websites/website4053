Gandhinagar

India's greenest capital city and a haven of peace, art, and spirituality.

History & Location

Located in the western part of India, Gandhinagar is the capital city of Gujarat. It is situated on the banks of the River Sabarmati and is known as the greenest capital in India.

Just 30 km away from Ahmedabad, it has emerged as a major tourist spot. It is believed to have been built by Sultan Ahmed Shah, who also constructed the city of Ahmedabad.

Major Attractions

Akshardham Temple

The most popular place in Gandhinagar, the Akshardham temple is a splendid work of art featuring beautiful carvings on pink stones. The temple is surrounded by lush green gardens and fresh water fountains.

Evening laser shows are organized at the temple and attract many tourists.

Adalaj Ni Vav

Located about 18 km south of Gandhinagar, this step-well was built by Rani Rudrabai in the 15th century. It is intricately carved with beautiful floral designs.

Nature & Parks

Indroda & Deer Park

Indroda Park (Deer Park) provides a peaceful escape from city life. It features a Dinosaurs Park with various statues, a python cage, an aquarium, and a snake park.

Recreation & Gardens

Sarita Garden on the banks of River Sabarmati is a favorite picnic spot. Punit Van in Sector 19 is a botanical garden that aligns plant species with zodiac signs.

Culture & Heritage

For a deeper look at local heritage, visit the craftsmen village at Pethapur. Other notable landmarks in the city include:

  • VitthalBhai Patel Bhavan: The domed Capital Complex.
  • Sector 28 Children's Park: Featuring a toy train and rides.
  • Gujarat Law University: A prominent academic site.
  • Nearby: The golden beaches of Daman and Diu.
