Rajkot: The Royal Soul

A commercial powerhouse and historical landmark, once the capital of the princely state of Saurashtra.

City Overview

Rajkot stands as a significant landmark in the backdrop of the Indian Independence movement. Situated on the banks of the Aji and Niari Rivers, it is famously known as the land of Mahatma Gandhi.

This vibrant city offers a perfect mix of education, historical museums, industries, and traditional crafts like Bandhani. Once the capital of the princely state of Saurashtra, Rajkot remains a primary commercial center of the region.

Climate & Best Time

Summer: 24°C to 42°C (Hot & Dry)

Winter: 10°C to 22°C (Pleasant)

Monsoon: Wet and prone to thunderstorms.

The winter season is the ideal time for tourism.

Top Tourist Sites

Kaba Gandhi's No Delo (Gandhi Smriti)

The ancestral home of Mahatma Gandhi, named after his father Karamchand "Kaba" Gandhi. Now a museum, it preserves personal memorabilia and a beautiful photo essay of the Mahatma's life through an arched pathway.

Watson Museum & Library

Located in Jubilee Gardens, it showcases Saurashtra's heritage, featuring artifacts from Mohenjodaro, 13th-century carvings, and a statue of Queen Victoria.

Rotary Dolls Museum

Explore global traditions through 1400 dolls from across the world. Established in 2001, it offers an entertaining learning experience for all ages.

Heritage & Recreation

Rashtriya Shala

Incepted in 1921 under Mahatma Gandhi's initiative, this institution was created to provide national education during the Non-Cooperation Movement.

Jubilee Garden

The city's central recreational park featuring colonial monuments like the Connaught Hall.

Libraries of Rajkot

The city boasts a rich literary tradition with major centers like the Lang Library and G.T. Sheth Library.

Must Visit

Wankaner Palace

An architectural marvel located near the city.

Jagat Mandir

A stunning temple dedicated to Shri Ramakrishna Paramhansa.

Picnic Spots

Lal Pari Lake, Aji Dam, and Randera offer picturesque settings.
