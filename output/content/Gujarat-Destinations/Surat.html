Surat Tour

The Silk City and Diamond Hub of India, where history and industry weave a rich tapestry.

City Snapshot

Area: 327 sq. km.
Altitude: 13 metres
Best Time: October to March
STD Code: (91) - 261

Historical Legacy

Established in 1496 by Hindu trader Mallik Gopi, Surat has been a prominent port city since ancient times. It became one of India's most prosperous cities during the late 17th and early 18th centuries, attracting merchants worldwide for its finest spices and silks.

Historical highlights include:

  • The establishment of the British East India Company's first warehouse in 1612.
  • Serving as the departure point for Mecca pilgrims during Mughal rule.
  • A transformation in the late 19th century into a flourishing industrial center, becoming the country's main textile market and a hub for petrochemicals and steel.

Major Sightseeing

Dandi

The site of Mahatma Gandhi's famous Salt Satyagraha, a monumental landmark in the Indian freedom struggle.

Bardoli

Located 34 km from Surat, home to the Swaraj Ashram where Gandhiji and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel launched the farmer's satyagraha in 1921.

Winchester Museum

Houses a rare collection of historical and geographical articles relating to Surat, some dating back centuries.

Bulsar (Vansada National Park)

A treat for wildlife enthusiasts, featuring leopards, tigers, panthers, and wild boars in their natural habitat.

Attractions & Projects

Ukai Dam

One of the largest dams in the country, matching the Bhakra Nangal in height and water storage capacity. Located 94 km from Surat.

Beaches & Resorts

Dumas seaside resort (16 km), palm-fringed Hajira beach (28 km), and Tithal beach (108 km) offer perfect getaways.

Old Fort

Built by Muhammed Tughlaq in the 14th century, this fort remains a significant historical landmark in the city.

Culture & Festivals

Surat is a melting pot of cultures, frequently hosting exchange programs like the Octave Festival, which showcases the traditions, music, food, and tapestry of eight north-eastern Indian states.

The city's Navratri celebrations and folk dances are a highlight of its vibrant cultural life.

How to Reach

By Air

Nearest airport is Vadodara. Daily flights from Delhi connect Vadodara/Surat.

By Rail

Major station on the Delhi-Ahmedabad-Mumbai route. Regular trains like Golden Temple Mail and Gujarat Mail.

By Road

Excellent road network connects with Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Delhi.
