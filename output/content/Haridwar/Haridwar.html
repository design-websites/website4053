Haridwar

The Gateway to God, where the holy Ganges begins its journey through the plains.

Spiritual Significance

Haridwar is one of the seven holiest places in Hinduism and serves as a major pilgrimage center in Uttarakhand. It is here that the River Ganges leaves the mountains to enter the Indo-Gangetic plains.

The city is a kaleidoscope of Indian culture and civilization. From the soul-stirring Ganga Aarti to the massive gathering of the Kumbh Mela, Haridwar remains an eternal favorite for seekers of peace and spiritual awakening.

Sacred Sites

Har Ki Pauri

The most famous ghat on the banks of the Ganges. It is believed that Lord Vishnu left his footprint here on a stone set into the upper wall of the ghats.

Mansa Devi Temple

Perched atop the Bilwa Parvat, this temple is dedicated to Goddess Mansa Devi. Visitors can reach it via a scenic cable car ride (Udan Khatola).

Chandi Devi Temple

Situated on Neel Parvat, this temple was built by the King of Kashmir. It is a highly revered Siddh Peetha where wishes are believed to be fulfilled.

Maya Devi Temple

Dating back to the 11th century, this temple is dedicated to the presiding deity of Haridwar and is one of the three Siddh Peethas in the city.

Unforgettable Experiences

Ganga Aarti

Witness the mesmerizing evening prayer at Har Ki Pauri, where thousands of lamps are floated on the river accompanied by rhythmic chants and bells.

Holy Dip

Taking a dip in the holy waters of the Ganges is believed to wash away sins and lead the devotee toward salvation (Moksha).

Travel Snapshot

Best Time

October to April is ideal. The weather is pleasant for sightseeing and temple visits.

Accessibility

Well-connected by rail to Delhi and Mumbai. Dehradun Jolly Grant Airport is the nearest air link.

Local Flavors

Don't miss the street food—hot jalebis, kachoris, and aloo puri are local favorites.
