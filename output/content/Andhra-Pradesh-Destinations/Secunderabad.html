Secunderabad

The historic twin city of Hyderabad, born of Nizam heritage and British military legacy.

Introduction to Secunderabad

Named after Sikander Jah, the third Nizam of the Asaf Jahi dynasty, Secunderabad is situated about 10 km east of its 'twin city,' Hyderabad. The two cities are beautifully connected by the road on the bund of the Hussain Sagar Lake.

Historically, the British maintained one of their largest military establishments here, and today the city remains a hub for historic monuments and cultural heritage.

Palaces & Official Residences

Rashtrapati Nilayam

Considered the equivalent of New Delhi's Rashtrapati Bhavan, it serves as the official office for the President of India during their yearly stopover in South India.

Falaknuma Palace

Built by Sir Vikar Ul Umra in an Italian architectural style, this majestic palace is globally applauded for its beauty. Its grand halls are notably studded with precious stones.

Purani Haveli

Constructed by Mir Mahboob Ali in 18th-century European style, it is famous for housing the world's longest wooden wardrobe.

Salarjung Museum

Built by Salarjung III, it houses prehistoric art and personal belongings of emperors like Aurangzeb, Tipu Sultan, and Shah Jahan.

Nature & Science

Nehru Zoological Park

Covering over 300 acres, it is one of Asia's largest parks. It features a lion safari and a unique nocturnal zoo where visitors can see creatures active only at night.

B.M. Birla Planetarium & Science Museum

A must-visit for astronomy enthusiasts. Set with advanced technologies, it offers concise views of the universe and enormous science facades.

Lumbini Park

A modern addition to the city, famous for its fascinating musical fountain show where water "dances" to curated music.

Spiritual & Educational Landmarks

  • Mecca Masjid: A sacred hall supported by beautiful arches, featuring octagonal columns made from a single piece of granite.
  • Trinity Church: Historically significant to British regiments, now maintained by the local church community.
  • Osmania University: Known for long corridors filled with Ellora paintings, Damascus-patterned gateways, and tranquil gardens.
  • Golconda Fort: A 13th-century Kakatiya marvel that later became a world-renowned center for the diamond trade.

Travel Snapshot

Connectivity: Linked to Hyderabad via the Hussain Sagar Tank Bund road.

Did you know? Secunderabad is known as a 'nocturnal zoo' hub due to the specific exhibits at the Nehru Zoological Park.

History Tip: The city's name honors Sikander Jah, the 3rd Nizam of the Asaf Jahi dynasty.
