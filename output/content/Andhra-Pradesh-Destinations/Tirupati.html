Home of Lord Venkateswara on the sacred peaks of Tirumala.

Glorious History

The glorious history of Tirupati Venkateswara Temple spans several centuries. Literary evidence and engraved inscriptions highlight its antiquity, showing regular visits from the Chola, Pandya, and Pallava dynasties.

Major contributors include Sri Krishnadevaraya, who endowed the temple with rich offerings, and Maratha general Rajhoji Bhonsle, who established a permanent endowment fund. After passing through Muslim and British supervision, the Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) Committee took over in 1933, followed by a Board of Trustees in 1951.

The Temple Experience

Located on the seventh peak of the Tirupati Hill, this ancient temple is emblematic of architectural brilliance. The outer Gopuram is inscribed with figures of Vaishnava gods.

Notable features include the Ranga Mandapam (Vijaynagara style), the Aina Mahal (Mirror Palace), and the Dhvajasthamba Mandapam, where central pillars emit musical sounds when struck. The portals are adorned with statues of Krishnadevaraya and his consorts.

Darshan & Pilgrim Systems

Vaikuntam Queue Complex

A series of clean, airy halls leading to the main temple. Facilities include subsidized food canteens, fresh milk, medical aid, and devotional CCTV broadcasts.

Sudharsanam Token System

Tokens are available free of cost at various locations including the Tirupati Railway Station choultries. These tokens indicate your darshan time, significantly reducing wait times.

Special Darshan

Includes categories for tickets (Rs. 40/50) and a dedicated gate at the Maha Dwaram for the physically disabled and the aged.

Pilgrim Facilities

Free Meals & Aid

Free full meals are provided from 10 AM to 11 PM with temple coupons. Free medical aid is available at Aswini Hospital.

Transport & Luggage

Free luggage transport to Tirumala. Four free buses operate within Tirumala covering major points.

Kalyanakatta

Facilities for fulfilling tonsure vows. Fixed tariff: Rs 2.00 at Kalyanakatta and Rs 10.00 at cottages.

Automobile Clinic

A mobile mechanics van with spares is available to assist vehicles stranded on the Ghat roads.

Connectivity

By Air

Renigunta Airport (15 km) connects to Hyderabad, Chennai, and Bengaluru.

By Rail

The main station is Tirupati. Daily trains from Mumbai, Puri, Varanasi, and Chennai.

By Road

Major links: Bengaluru (248 km), Chennai (151 km), and Hyderabad (617 km). Use SVBS bus station if arriving by train.
