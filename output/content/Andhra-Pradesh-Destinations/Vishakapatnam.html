Introduction to Vizag

Visakhapatnam, popularly known as Vizag, is situated between the hills of the Eastern Ghats and the Bay of Bengal. Named after Visakha, the god of bravery, it has transformed from a petite fishing town into a mega-industrial hub and one of the finest natural harbors in Asia.

The city is enclosed by three hills which house shrines of major religions, while the Godavari and Indravati rivers flow proudly through the region.

Major Attractions

Kailashagiri

A hilltop park offering striking views of the Bay of Bengal and the entire city. It is accessible by ropeway and is a charming destination for all travelers.

Simhachalam Temple

A marvel constructed by the Gajapathi Kings of Orissa. Dedicated to Lord Narasimha, it features intricately carved pavilions including the 96-pillared Kalyana Mantapa.

Submarine Museum

Explore INS Kursura, Asia's first submarine museum. It stands as a major attraction on the beach road for technical and history enthusiasts.

Dolphin's Nose

A massive 357m high rock named for its resemblance to a dolphin's nose. This landmark features a powerful lighthouse and is a travel essential.

Nature & Wildlife

Indira Gandhi Zoological Park

Spanning 250 hectares on the Beach Road, this park is enclosed by hills and the sea. It houses approximately 700 animals from 89 different species.

Pristine Beaches

The beautiful Rishikonda and Ramakrishna Beaches are massive visitor magnets, perfect for relaxation and seaside activities.

Beyond the City

Araku Valley

Located at a height of 1000m outside the city, this hill station presents captivating views with scenic gardens, waterfalls, and mountain streams.

Travel Snapshot

Climate

Tropical Savanna climate. Temperatures range between 23°C and 32°C year-round.

Best Time to Visit

November to February is ideal for moderate temperatures and minimal rain.

Key Facts

Developed by the British as a strategic port to export mineral wealth. It remains one of the largest industrial hubs in Andhra Pradesh.
