Vijayawada: The Place of Victory

A bustling coastal city of business, ancient penance, and legendary triumph.

At a Glance

Altitude: 125 Metres
Best Time: Sept to Feb (Dasara)
Languages: Urdu, English
Summer: 28°C - 47°C
Winter: 15°C - 30°C
Also Known As: Bezawada

History of Vijayawada

Vijayawada literally translates to "The Place of Victory". Legend says Arjuna, the Pandava prince, offered prayers atop the Indrakeeladri Hill and won the blessings of Lord Shiva.

Bounded by the Indrakiladri hills and the Budameru River, the city flourished during the British rule with the completion of the Krishna Barrage. It is a cosmopolitan hub today, historically visited by the famous Chinese traveler Hieun T’sang.

Major Attractions

Kanaka Durga Temple

The presiding deity of the city, set on Indrakeeladri Hill. This self-manifested (Swayambhu) goddess is a symbol of power and riches.

Prakasam Barrage

A 1223.5m long modern regulator stretching across the Krishna. Its canals give the city a unique "Venetian" appearance.

Undavalli Caves

Located 8 km from the city, these high hills host fine specimens of cut-in architectural and sculptural models overlooking the river.

Bhavani Island

A picturesque island located upstream on the Krishna River, serving as a fantastic picnic spot for holiday-lovers.

Excursions & Surroundings

Amaravati (64 km)

Famous for the temple of Lord Amareswara and world-renowned Buddhist sculptures and excavations.

Kuchipudi (60 km)

The birthplace of Siddhendra Yogi, originator of the world-famous Kuchipudi dance style. Features a full-fledged dance school.

Manginapudi Beach

Located near Machilipatnam, this natural bay features safe waters and a beautifully laid park with fountains.

Travel Guide

By Air

Gannavaram Airport (20 km) connects to Hyderabad (30-min flight) and Visakhapatnam.

By Rail

The largest junction of South Central Railway. Direct links to Chennai, Delhi, Howrah, and Hyderabad.

By Road

Major transit point via NH5 and NH9. KSRTC provides services to all regional hubs.
