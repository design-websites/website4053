Guntur City Trip

Explore the abundance of nature's treasures, ancient rock-cut caves, and historic forts.

Introduction

Guntur holds a pride of place, blessed with an abundance of nature's treasures such as hills, valleys, rivers, and beaches. It was an important region where Buddhism flourished in the early centuries.

The city's cosmopolitan culture, historical and modern artifacts, picturesque locales, and delicious food have made Guntur an increasingly popular tourist destination.

Major Attractions

Nagarjuna Sagar Dam

Located 150 km from Guntur, this is the largest masonry dam in the world with a height of 124 meters. Its sheer magnitude and picturesque surroundings are breathtaking.

Ethipothala Falls

Formed on the Chandravanka River, a stream of the Krishna, these waterfalls cascade 70 feet into a lagoon amidst beautiful scenic surroundings.

Undavalli Caves

Ancient four-storied rock-cut temples and caves on the banks of the river Krishna. They represent a classic mix of Hindu and Buddhist cultures and architecture.

Mangalagiri

Famous for the Lakshmi Narasimha temple. Devotees believe the deity accepts only half the quantity of 'Panaka' (sweet drink) offered to him.

Historic & Religious Sites

Kondaveedu Fort

Built during the reign of the Reddy Kings around the 10th century A.D. It features 21 stupas and offers natural scenic beauty ideal for trekking.

Macherla & Karampudi

Macherla is renowned for the Chennakesava Swamy temple built by Hiheya Kings. Karampudi also houses a historic Chennakesava Swamy temple where weapons from the Palnadu war are preserved.

Unique Temples

Visit Chebrole to see the rare Chaturmukha Brahma Devalayam, or Sitanagaram for the Someswara Swamy temple and its vast sandy beach.

Nature & Recreation

Uppalapadu Bird Sanctuary

Home to 40 species of migratory birds from Siberia, including Pelicans and Ibises. The village water tanks are refuge for spot-billed pelicans.

NTR Manasa Sarovaram

A beautiful recreation center spread over 55 acres. It is exotically landscaped with flowerbeds, lawns, ponds, and small hillocks.
