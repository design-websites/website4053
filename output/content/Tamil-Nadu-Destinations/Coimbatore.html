Overview of Coimbatore

Coimbatore is the second largest city of Tamil Nadu, famously known as the "Manchester of South India" due to its extensive textile industry. Situated on the banks of the Noyyal River, the city serves as a hub for cotton production, supported by the region's rich black alluvial soil.

Historical Legacy

Dating back to the 2nd Century A.D., the region was originally a tribal territory known as Kongunad. Over centuries, it was ruled by the Cholas, Pandyas, and the Vijayanagara Empire.

In 1804, it was established as a capital district under the British administration, playing a prominent role in the Second Poligar War of 1801.

Top Sightseeing Spots

Anamalai Sanctuary

A sprawling wildlife haven at 1,400 meters, home to elephants, tigers, and diverse bird species.

Marudamalai Temple

A hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Subrahmanya, famous for the Thai Possam festival.

Siruvani Waterfalls

Renowned for its scenic beauty and mineral-rich water, believed to have healing powers.

Black Thunder

One of Asia's premier amusement parks, set against the backdrop of the Nilgiri hills.
