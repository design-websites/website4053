Ooty Tour

The Queen of Hill Stations in the Nilgiris.

Overview & History

Ooty was developed by John Sullivan as a summer retreat for British officials. Today, it is famous for its tea estates, which produce one of India's favorite drinks and add to the resort's charm. It offers solitude and mesmerizing nature away from city life.

Top Sightseeing

Botanical Garden

Spread over 50 acres, it features amazing flora and an annual summer flower show in May.

Ooty Lake

An artificial lake built in 1824, offering boating, fishing, and a nearby toy train ride.

Doddabetta Peak

The highest point in Tamil Nadu (2,623m), offering panoramic views through its Telescope House.

Centenary Rose Garden

Covers 10 acres with more than 1,000 varieties of roses.

Getting There

Air: Coimbatore Airport (88km).

Rail: Ooty Railway Station (2km from center); Mettupalayam toy train is a highlight.
