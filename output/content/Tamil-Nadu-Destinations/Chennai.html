History & Overview

Formerly known as Madras, Chennai originated as a tiny fishing village. It has been ruled by the Pallava, Chola, Pandya, and Vijaynagar empires. In 1639, Francis Day acquired land to build Fort St. George, which became the nucleus of the colonial city.

Today, Chennai is India's fourth largest metropolitan city and a major commercial, industrial, and cultural center known for its temple architecture.

Top Sightseeing Spots

Fort St. George

Founded by the British, it now hosts the Tamil Nadu Secretariat.

Snake Park

A central location for breeding endangered pythons, founded by Romulus Whitaker.
