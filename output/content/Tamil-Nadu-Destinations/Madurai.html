Madurai Tour

Step into the ancient Lotus-shaped city of Madhurapuri.

Quick Facts

Climate: Summer (26°C - 40°C), Winter (18°C - 29°C). Dry and hot weather dominates.

The City of Temples

Madurai, the capital of Pandya kings, is one of the oldest inhabited cities. Legend says Lord Shiva showered divine nectar (Madhu) on the city, giving it the name Madhurapuri.

Major Attractions

Sri Meenakshi Temple

A 2000-year-old architectural marvel spanning 65,000 sq m, dedicated to Parvati.

Thirumalai Nayak Palace

Built in 1636, famous for its Indo-Saracenic style and massive black stone pillars.

Alagar Koil

Situated on Solaimalai hill, this temple is dedicated to Lord Alagar (Vishnu).

Goripalayam Dargah

A prominent Muslim site on the banks of Vaigai river with a distinct green dome.
