Rameswaram Tour

The Varanasi of the South and an Engineering Wonder.

Overview

Rameswaram is an island shaped like Vishnu's Conch. It is where Lord Rama offered thanks to Shiva. The town is connected by the Indira Gandhi Bridge, an engineering marvel.

Sightseeing

Ramanathaswamy Temple

One of the twelve Jyotirlingas, featuring 22 wells with water of different tastes and curative properties.

DhanushKodi

Named after Lord Rama's bow, this tip of the island was largely destroyed in 1964 but remains a spiritual site.

Erwadi

A famous Muslim pilgrim spot housing the tomb of Ibrahim Sahid Auliya.

Getting There

Rail: Directly connected to Chennai, Madurai, and Trichy.

Air: Madurai Airport (167km) is the nearest.
