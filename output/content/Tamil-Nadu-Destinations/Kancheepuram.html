Kancheepuram Tour

The City of a Thousand Temples and World-Famous Silk.

The Holy City of Kanchi

Kancheepuram is one of the most holy pilgrim centers for Hindus. It is admired for its prominent hand-woven silk sarees and its rich culture and civilization dating back centuries.

Places of Interest

Kailasanathar Temple

Built in typical Pallava style, famous for exquisite carvings and its pyramidal tower.

Ekambareswarar Temple

Features a 57m high Raja Gopuram and a 2,500-year-old mango tree.

Kanchi Kamakshi Temple

A beautiful temple dedicated to the goddess Kamakshi, an embodiment of Parvati, Lakshmi, and Saraswati.

Handloom Industry

Explore the source of pure mulberry silk sarees, known for their luster and durability.
