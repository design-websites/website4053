Mudumalai Wildlife Sanctuary

South India's First Wildlife Reserve

Sanctuary Details

Area

32,155 Hectares

Altitude

350 - 1266m

Best Time

Feb to June

Temp

4.8°C - 24.3°C

Wildlife & Fauna

Mudumalai houses one of the largest elephant populations in India. Other inhabitants include tigers, leopards, hyenas, sloth bears, and the Nilgiri Tahr. Bird watchers can spot over 200 species, including the Crested Hawk Eagle and Malabar Grey Hornbill.

Flora

The terrain is a mix of tropical evergreen forests, moist teak forests, and grassy woodlands. Rosewood and Teak are the most valuable timber trees found here, alongside sandalwood, mango, and banyan trees.

How to Reach

Air: Coimbatore Airport (160km).

Rail: Udhagamandalam Railway Station (65km).

Road: Gudalur is the nearest town (16km).
