Thanjavur Tour

The Chola Capital and the Granary of South India.

A Center for Classical Arts

Thanjavur remains a global center for classical arts, music, and the unique Tanjore style of painting. The city is defined by its massive architectural achievements, primarily from the Chola dynasty.

Places of Interest

Brahadeeswarar (Big Temple)

A magnum opus of Chola architecture. The temple stands 216 ft tall and is designed so its shadow never falls on the ground.

Saraswathi Mahal Library

Established around 1700 AD, it contains over 44,000 palm leaf and paper manuscripts.

Royal Palace

A magnificent complex featuring a seven-storied observation tower for the royal family.

Rajagopala Beerangi

The biggest cannon in India, speaking to the incredible metallurgical knowledge of the era.
