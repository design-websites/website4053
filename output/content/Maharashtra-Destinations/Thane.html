Thane: The City of Lakes

Historically known as 'Sristhanaka' and the historic terminus of India's first railway.

Regional Overview

Thane district is situated in the northwest part of Maharashtra. Known as the Abode of Lord Ganesha, it is bordered by Nashik and Ahmadnagar to the east, Raigad to the south, Mumbai to the north, and Pune to the southeast. It serves as a booming industrial town with rapidly growing trade and construction activities.

Terminus of India's first railway
Former Portuguese trading center (until 1739)
Home to approximately 30 lakes
Divided by the scenic Thane Creek

Waterways & Recreation

Thane's identity is defined by its water bodies, offering opportunities for boating and water scooters.

Upvan Lake

Nestled in the foothills of Yeour hills and surrounded by the lofty Neelkanth, it is a popular hangout for youth and families seeking scenic tranquility.

Talopali

A frequent meeting point for all ages. Visitors enjoy rowing, water scooters, and local roadside eateries serving tangy Bhel and ice cream.

Major Rivers

  • Vaitarna: Flows across Shahapur, Wade, and Palghar.
  • Ulhas: Originates from Borghat, eventually meeting the Arabian Sea at Vasai Creek.

Places of Interest

Yeour Hills

Also known as Mama Bhanja Hills, this is a haven for nature lovers and wild animals. It houses the Swami Math, a perfect retreat for peace seekers.

Tikujiniwadi

A premier picnic spot set in the backwoods, featuring an in-house restaurant and a full day of recreational activities in the lap of nature.

Hill Stations

The district features naturally beautiful, pollution-free hill stations like Jawahar and Suryamal.

Nearby Escapes

Easily accessible attractions include Essel World, Water Kingdom, Matheran, Tansa Wildlife Sanctuary, and Jawhar Palace.

Travel Guide

Climate

Hot and humid weather with temperatures between 32°C and 34°C. Mountainous regions receive more rainfall than the coast.

Other Notable Lakes

  • Masunda Lake
  • Kachrali Lake
  • Makhmali Lake
  • Siddheshwar Lake
  • Raila Devi Lake

Distance Hub

Close proximity to Mumbai, providing a gateway to North Maharashtra.
