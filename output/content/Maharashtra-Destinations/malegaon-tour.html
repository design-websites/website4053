Malegaon City Trip

A historic textile center at the confluence of the Mousam and Girna rivers.

Location and History

Malegaon, also known as Taluka Loha, is located in the Nasik district of Maharashtra at the meeting point of the Mousam and Girna rivers. It is the largest city in the district after Nasik itself. The town became a significant refuge for North Indian Muslims after the 1857 revolt and remains a cultural hub for Muslim communities today.

57 km from Nanded
Famous for Textile & Power Loom industries
Base for the Maratha film industry
Home to ~300 Mosques

Major Attractions

Malegaon Fort

Constructed by a Maratha ruler in the 18th century on the banks of the Mousam river. It uniquely features a shrine on top and a temple below, reflecting the secular history of the region.

Urdu Library

A 60-year-old modern institution with a vast collection of works from renowned Urdu authors covering literature, history, health, and education.

Jamiatus Swalehaat

The largest institution of Islamic education for women in India. Its global reputation attracts students from all over the world.

Indian Muslim Heritage

Visit the Shahi Masjid, the oldest mosque in the city, or the 40-acre largest cemetery in India.

Industry and Media

Malegaon has gained national attention through the documentary Supermen of Malegaon, highlighting the town's vibrant film-loving culture.

Textile Hub: Exploring the thriving power loom workshops is a popular activity for visitors.
Film Studios: The local Maratha film industry sets and studios are major tourist draws.
