Solapur: The Gateway of Culture

A historic city where Marathi, Kannada, and Telugu traditions converge in a vibrant tapestry.

Overview

Solapur is a beautiful mix of Kannada, Marathi, and Telugu culture. The city holds a unique place in history for enjoying freedom three days before India officially gained independence. It is also a land of saints and significant religious heritage.

Moderate Climate: Comfortable temperatures throughout the year.
Best Time: October to March is ideal for visiting.
Famed Industry: World-renowned for durable 'Solapur Chaddars'.
Hutatma Chowk: A memorial for brave freedom fighters.

Spiritual Landmarks

Solapur is surrounded by some of the most revered pilgrimage sites in India.

Pandharpur

The supreme pilgrimage spot, home to Sant Dnyaneshwar and Tukaram. It is famous for the Waari festival held annually on Ashadi and Kartik Ekadashi.

Siddeshwar Temple

The primary temple of the city, along with Revansiddeshwar and historic temples in Karmala, Barshi, and Mangalwedha.

Jain Pilgrimage

Significant Jain temples including Mallikarjuna, Parasnath, and Adinath Temples are major draws for pilgrims.

Akkalkot

The Swami Maharaj Math, the Samadhi of the spiritual leader Swami Samarth Maharaja, is located here.

Nature and Industry

Wildlife & Nature

Explore the Great Indian Bustard Sanctuary or visit the Kambar Talao (Motibaug tank), which hosts thousands of migratory birds during winter.

Industrial Fame

Solapur is famous for its unique bedding accessories known as Solapur Chaddars, produced by numerous small and medium-scale industries.

Quick Travel Info

Geography

Situated near the western coasts of the Arabian Sea in South-Western Maharashtra.

Summer Temps

Maximum temperatures during the summer reach up to 36°C.

Winter Temps

Winter temperatures typically vary between 18°C and 23°C.

Must Visit

  • Hutatma Chowk Memorial
  • Motibaug Bird Sanctuary
  • Local Textile Markets
