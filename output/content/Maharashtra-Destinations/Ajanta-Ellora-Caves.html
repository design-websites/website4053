Ajanta & Ellora Caves

Ancient hand-cut masterpieces of Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain rock architecture.

Historical Marvels

The Ajanta and Ellora caves are two of India's most iconic destinations, featuring hand-carved rock structures. Founded in 1819 by British Officer John Smith during a hunting expedition, these caves stand as a testament to ancient artistic brilliance.

Ajanta: 29 Caves (200BC - 650AD)
Ellora: 34 Caves (350AD - 700AD)
Near Aurangabad, Maharashtra
Ajanta: UNESCO World Heritage Site

The Ajanta Caves

Ajanta features 30 caves, including 5 prayer halls (Chaityas) and 25 monasteries (Viharas), cut into a horseshoe-shaped ravine.

Key Artistic Highlights

  • Cave 1: Features the famous Bodhisattva Padmapani and Vajrapani paintings.
  • Cave 2: Renowned for the "Thousand Buddhas" painting and the princess on a swing.
  • Cave 11: Shows the transition from Hinayana to Mahayana worship with Buddha images attached to a stupa.
  • Cave 17: Contains strict interior arrangements and the Shad-Danta Jataka paintings.

The Ellora Caves

Locally known as 'Veru Lani', Ellora is the largest monolithic excavation in the world.

The Kailasa Temple (Cave 16)

The centerpiece of Ellora, started by Krishna I of the Rashtrakutha dynasty. It is a Dravidian art achievement featuring a Nandi Mandap and a main Shiva temple.

The Dashavatara (Cave 15)

Features a two-storeyed temple with panels illustrating the ten avatars of Vishnu, including the death of Hiranyakashipu.

Carpenter's Cave (Cave 10)

The most famous Buddhist cave, also known as Vishwakarma Cave, featuring a 15-foot seated Buddha.

River Goddesses (Cave 21)

Known as Ramesvara cave, marked by the presence of goddesses Ganga and Yamuna at the entrance.

How to Reach

By Air

Aurangabad (30km from Ellora, 99km from Ajanta) is linked to Delhi, Jaipur, and Mumbai.

By Rail

Aurangabad connects to Mumbai and Pune. Jalgaon is 59km from Ajanta.

By Road

Regular state buses from Mumbai, Pune, Nasik, Hyderabad, and more reach Aurangabad.

Travel Tip

Best time to visit is anytime of the year. MTDC operates tours from Mumbai.
