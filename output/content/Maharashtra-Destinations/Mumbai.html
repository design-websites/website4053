Mumbai City Trip

The "Film City of India" where modern heritage meets the shores of the Arabian Sea.

City Overview

Mumbai, formerly known as Bombay, derived its name from "Mumbadevi," the patron goddess of the Koli fisher folks. As the capital of Maharashtra, it is a bustling megalopolis where roughly 50% of the population consists of non-Maharashtrian groups like Gujaratis, South Indians, Parsis, and Sindhis.

State: Maharashtra
Climate: Humid (Summer Max 34°C)
Best Time: October to February
Marathi, Hindi, English, Gujarati

History

Upon India's independence in 1947, Bombay became the capital of Bombay State. In 1960, the state was dissolved, leading to the creation of Maharashtra with Bombay as its capital. The city officially changed its name to Mumbai in 1995.

Major Landmarks

Gateway of India

Built to honor the visit of King George V and Queen Mary. Today, it is a grand symbol of the city and the primary starting point for tourists.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus

Formerly Victoria Terminus, this historic railway station serves as the headquarters of Central Railway and is one of the busiest in India.

Haji Ali Mosque

Located in the middle of the sea, this mosque honors saint Haji Ali. It is accessible via a narrow path only during low tide.

Flora Fountain

A beautifully adorned stone fountain in the Fort area, featuring a statue of the Roman Goddess of Flowers, Flora.

Culture & Cuisine

Mumbai is a melting pot of ethnic backgrounds, famous for its unique "Bambaiyaa Hindi" dialect and Indo-western culture.

Street Food

The city's soul lies in its roadside fast food, specifically Vada Pav and Bhelpuri, alongside Chinese and South Indian staples.

Beverages

Tea is the most popular drink, served at numerous stalls across every corner, followed by coffee, coconut water, and fruit juices.

Travel Guide

By Air

Mumbai has extensive flight connectivity to major global cities, often exceeding New Delhi in traffic.

By Rail

Connected via Central (East/North) and Western (North India) Railways through major hubs like CST and Churchgate.

By Road

Highly accessible via National Highways 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, and 17, linking it to all parts of India.

Local Spots

  • Powai Lake (Picnic spot)
  • Aarey Milk Colony
  • Nehru Centre & Planetarium
  • Vasai Fort (Strategic Ruins)
