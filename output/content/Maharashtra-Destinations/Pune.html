Pune: The Motor City

A historic powerhouse of the Maratha Empire and India's thriving educational and industrial heart.

Overview

Pune, formerly known as Poona, is the 8th largest metropolis in India[cite: 587, 589]. It is the 2nd largest city in Maharashtra after Mumbai[cite: 587, 589]. Situated 560 meters above sea level, it is located at the junction of the Mula and Mutha rivers[cite: 589].

Education Hub: Features world-class facilities attracting students from India and abroad[cite: 590].
Motor City: Known for its widespread development of the automobile industry[cite: 598].
Tech Center: A rapidly growing center for Information Technology[cite: 589, 599].
Maratha History: Once a major power center of the Maratha Empire[cite: 588, 589].

History

Pune established itself as a town as early as 937 AD[cite: 591]. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, founder of the Maratha Empire, spent his youth here[cite: 592]. Following his coronation in 1674, he contributed significantly to the development of the area[cite: 593].

In 1720, Baji Rao was appointed Prime Minister of the Maratha Empire and made Pune his base[cite: 594]. From 1837 until independence, the city served as the monsoon capital of the Bombay Presidency[cite: 595].

Major Attractions

Museums & Culture

  • Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum: A prominent local museum[cite: 599].
  • National War Museum: Honoring military history[cite: 599].
  • Tribal Museum: Showcasing indigenous heritage[cite: 599].
  • Sawai Gandharva Sangeet Mahotsav: A significant Indian classical music festival held annually in December[cite: 601].

Public Parks & Academies

  • Rajiv Gandhi Zoological Park: Located close to the city[cite: 600].
  • College of Military Engineering: A notable training institution[cite: 601].
  • Mahatma Phule Museum: Important cultural site[cite: 599].
  • Babasaheb Ambedkar Museum: Dedicated memorial museum[cite: 599].

Industrial Powerhouse

Pune's status as the "Motor City of India" is driven by industry leaders[cite: 598]:

Automotive Giants

Home to Tata Motors, Force Motors, Mercedes Benz, Volkswagen, Fiat, and General Motors[cite: 596, 597].

IT & Forging

Features the Hinjawadi IT Park (Rajiv Gandhi Park) and major forging industries[cite: 589, 599].

How to Reach

By Air

Pune International Airport is situated at Lohegaon[cite: 603].

By Rail

Connected via express trains to Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Howrah, and Jamshedpur[cite: 605].

Local Travel

EMU local trains link the city to neighboring hill stations[cite: 604].
