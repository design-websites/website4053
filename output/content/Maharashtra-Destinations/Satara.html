Satara: City of Seven Hills

The historic capital of the Marathas, where the Sahyadri ranges meet the sacred Krishna River[cite: 612].

Overview

Surrounded by seven hills, Satara is a natural gift in West Maharashtra located near the confluence of the River Krishna[cite: 612]. Once a capital of the Marathas, it is surrounded by Pune to the north, Solapur to the east, Sangli to the south, and Ratnagiri to the west[cite: 612, 613].

Topography: Sahyadri and Mahadeo ranges stretch through the district[cite: 614].
Major Rivers: Krishna (sacred river) and its largest tributary, the Koyna[cite: 615, 616].
Temp: Varies between 11.6°C and 37.5°C[cite: 623].
Avg. Rainfall: 1426 mm[cite: 623].

History & Heritage

Satara has a rich historical background; it is believed that Wai (then known as 'Viratnagari') provided temporary shelter to the Pandavas during their 13th year of exile[cite: 621, 622].

The land is famous for many renowned personalities[cite: 617]:

  • Zanshichi Rani Laxmibai, the famed fighter[cite: 618].
  • Savitribai Phule, the first lady teacher in India[cite: 619].
  • Dr. Karmaveer Bhaurao Patil, founder of 'Rayat Shikshan Sanstha'[cite: 620].
  • Krantisinh Nana Patil, freedom fighter[cite: 618].
  • Yeshawantrao Chavan, the first Chief Minister of Maharashtra[cite: 619].

Major Attractions

Ajinkyatara Fort

Built by Bhojraj II, this 3300-foot-high fort offers amazing views of the city[cite: 628, 629]. It houses water tanks and temples of Devi Mangalai, Lord Shiva, and Lord Hanuman[cite: 630].

Thoseghar Water Fall

A spectacular 1000-foot-high waterfall that serves as a famous picnic spot, especially during the rainy season[cite: 631, 632].

Chalkewadi

Rightfully quoted as the "Garden of Windmills," this site features hundreds of windmills producing thousands of megawatts of electricity[cite: 633].

Kuraneshwar Temple

This historical temple of 'Swyambhu Ganesh' is the 'Gram daivat' of Satara and features temples for both Lord Ganesh and Lord Shiva[cite: 625, 626].

Other Points of Interest

Satara is also a gateway to the beautiful hill stations of Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani[cite: 624]. Other notable sites include:

Yevateshwar Temple [cite: 634]
Shivaji Museum [cite: 634]
Kas Lake [cite: 634]
Wai Ganpati Temple [cite: 634]
Char Bhinti Smarak [cite: 634]
Shri Bhavani Museum [cite: 634]

Travel Guide

Climate & Vegetation

Rainfall is unequal; heavy in Mahabaleshwar but very low in Tehsil Man[cite: 623]. Vegetation varies from monsoon forests in the west to poor scrub in the east[cite: 623].

Strategic Importance

Ajinkyatara was historically a vital fort used by rulers to monitor entire South Maharashtra[cite: 630].

Kuraneshwar Highlights

The Kuraneshwar temple area is home to playing rabbits and brightly coloured peacocks for visitors to enjoy[cite: 627].
