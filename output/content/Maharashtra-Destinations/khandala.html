Khandala Hill Station

A mist-covered jewel in the Western Ghats, perfect for weekend escapes.

Gateway to the Ghats

Located at the top end of Bhor Ghat, Khandala serves as a critical road link between the Deccan Plateau and the Konkan plain. This scenic hill station in the Western Ghats is a favorite weekend destination for those traveling from Mumbai and Pune.

3 km from Lonavala
Part of Sahyadri Mountain Range
Popular Health & Hill Resort
Famous for Chikki varieties

Major Points of Interest

Duke’s Nose

A majestic cliff resembling the nose of the Duke of Wellington. It offers breathtaking panoramic views of the surrounding valleys and forests.

Tiger’s Leap

A viewpoint creating the illusion of a leaping tiger across the valley. It is also known for the distinctive echo produced by dropping rocks into its crevasse.

Lohgad Fort

Famous for its refreshing waterfalls and ancient rock-cut caves located atop a steep flight of steps.

Korigad Fort

Located in Amby Valley at 3050 ft. above sea level. Visitors trek from the village of Shahpur through rugged hills to reach this historic site.

Attractions Around Khandala

  • Bhushi & Valvan Dams
  • Kaivalyadhama Yoga Hospital
  • Ryewood Park
  • Karla & Bhaja Caves

The rock-cut Karla and Bhaja caves represent some of the oldest examples of Early Buddhist temple art in India.

Travel Guide

By Air

Pune Airport is the nearest at a distance of 66 km.

By Rail

Connected via trains from Mumbai Central and Pune Junction; all trains halt at Khandala.

By Road

Via Mumbai-Pune Expressway.
Mumbai to Lonavala: 104 km.
Pune to Lonavala: 66 km.
