Kaziranga National Park

A picturesque sanctuary and the proud home of the Great One-Horned Rhino.

History & Conservation

Kaziranga National Park, situated in the northeastern state of Assam, is one of the most picturesque wildlife parks in India. It is the natural habitat of the one-horned rhino (Rhinoceros unicornis) and lies on the southern banks of the Brahmaputra River.

Originally established as a game reserve in 1908, it was declared a sanctuary in 1940 to counter excessive poaching. In 1974, the Indian Government declared it a protected site.

The park's original inhabitants—the rhino and the elephant—now thrive in a serene environment. Its vast open spaces and the presence of the mighty Brahmaputra and Mikir hills make it a complete 'jungle' adventure.

Major Wildlife Attractions

The Big Five & More

Beside the one-horned Rhino, the park hosts large populations of Indian Elephants, Indian Bison, Swamp Deer (Barasingha), Sloth Bears, and Tigers. You may also see Leopard Cats, Hoolock Gibbons, and Wild Buffalo.

Birding Paradise

A raptor country featuring the Pallas's Fishing Eagle and Himalayan Griffon. In winter, huge numbers of migratory birds like Greylag Geese and Ruddy Shelduck descend on the park's lakes.

Elephant Safari

The vast open country makes wildlife viewing fairly pleasurable. Visitors can leave in the early hours of dawn for an elephant-back ride led by authorized and trained Mahouts.

This experience provides a chance to see animals in the wild at close quarters, including wild elephant herds and rhinos browsing past visitors unconcernedly.

Sightseeing & Local Culture

  • Tea & Plantations: Venture through the famous tea gardens of Assam or stroll through lush coffee and rubber plantations in nearby Karbi Anglong.

  • Karbi Villages: Visit Karbi villages to meet the people and observe their unique way of living.

How to Reach

By Air

Guwahati (217 km away) is the nearest airport. Jorhat is another option at 97 km from the park.

By Rail

The nearest railhead is Furkating, situated 75 km away from the National Park.

By Road

The main gate is at Kohora on NH-37. State (ASTC) and private buses stop here on their way to Guwahati, Tezpur, and Upper Assam.
