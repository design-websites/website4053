Guwahati: Heaven on Earth

A mystic land of eternal blue hills, beautiful rivers, and sacred shrines.

The City of Eastern Light

Guwahati, the capital of Assam, derives its name from the Assamese words ‘Guwa’ (areca nut) and ‘Haat’ (market). Historically known as ‘Pragjyotishpur’, the city is perched on the eastern edges of the Himalayan Mountains.

Situated on the banks of the mighty Brahmaputra River, it serves as the largest industrial, commercial, and educational hub of North East India. It is a land famous for its tea, mystic rivers, and serene blue hills.

Spiritual Landmarks

Kamakhya Temple

Perched on Nilachal Hill, this 10th-century shrine built by King Naranarayan is one of the world's premier tantric sites for Shakti worship. Animal sacrifice remains a tradition here.

Umananda (Shiva) Temple

Located on an island in the middle of the Brahmaputra River. Reachable via motor boats and public ferries from Umananda Ghat.

Navagraha Temple

Known as the "Temple of Nine Planets," this hilltop shrine is an ancient center for astrology and astronomy.

Sri Balaji Temple

A magnificent temple spread over two acres at Betkuchi, reflecting the architectural grandeur of the South in the heart of the North East.

Culture & Heritage

Srimanta Sankardeva Kalakshetra

A multi-arts complex named after the Vaishnava saint and integrator Srimanta Sankardeva, showcasing the diverse culture of the Assamese people.

Vashistha Ashram

Established by Sage Vashistha, this ashram is surrounded by greenery and the three rivulets: Lalita, Kanta, and Sandhya.

Bhubaneshwari Temple

Situated high above the Kamakhya Temple, providing an awesome panoramic view of the entire city of Guwahati.
