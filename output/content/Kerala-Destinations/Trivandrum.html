Trivandrum: Capital of Kerala

Built over seven hills, discover the tourist-friendly "City of Anantha."

Quick Facts

Area: 2,192 sq km
Altitude: Sea level
Best Time: October to April
Rainfall: 170 cms (May-Nov)
Languages: Malayalam, Tamil, English
STD Code: 0471

History & Overview

Trivandrum is the anglicised form of Thiruvananthapuram, the capital city of Kerala. Built over seven hills, it is popularly known as the 'City of Anantha', referring to the sacred serpent in Hindu mythology.

The city serves as a major gateway to southern India with its own international airport. Historically and socio-economically, it maintains strong connections with the Middle East, Sri Lanka, and the Maldives.

Major Attractions

Padmanabhaswamy Temple

Built in Dravidian style with walls ornate with beautiful murals. It features an image of Lord Vishnu reclining on the annular snake, Anantha.

Kovalam Beach

A major tourist magnet with silvery sands and swaying palms. Features include the Lighthouse Beach, adventure sports, and numerous yoga centers.

Kuthiramalika Palace Museum

Built by Maharajah Swathi Thirunal Balarama Varma, this museum houses a rare collection of wood carvings, miniature paintings, and antiques.

Sri Chitra Art Gallery

Located near the Napier Museum, it displays works by Raja Ravi Varma and art from the Rajput, Tanjore, and Mughal dynasties.

Popular Excursions

Kanyakumari (87 Km)

The southern-most part of India where the Arabian Sea, Indian Ocean, and Bay of Bengal meet. Famous for sunset views and religious significance.

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

A favorite haunt for migratory birds like the Siberian stork. Boat cruises on Vembanad Lake are the ideal way to spot resident herons and egrets.

Thiruvallam (10 Km)

A serene backwater stretch perfect for quiet houseboat and canoe rides.

Travel Guide

By Air

International airport connected to Dubai, Colombo, and Kuwait. Daily domestic flights to Delhi, Mumbai, and Chennai.

By Rail

Major rail junction at Thampanoor. The Kerala Express connects daily to New Delhi.

By Road

Central bus stand at Thampanoor. KSRTC connects to Kochi, Kollam, and Kanyakumari.

Distance to Major Cities

  • Kochi: 220 Km
  • Madurai: 264 Km
  • Coimbatore: 413 Km
  • Bangalore: 753 Km
