Cochin: Queen of the Arabian Sea

A modernized commercial hub where frozen-in-time colonial charm meets contemporary life.

Quick Facts

Area: 309.22 km²
Altitude: 8 meters
Best Time: November to March
Rainfall: 254 cm (June-Oct)
Languages: Malayalam, English
STD Code: 0484

Overview & History

Cochin (Kochi) is the commercial capital and one of the most modernized cities in Kerala. It is an aggregation of islands and narrow peninsulas, with Ernakulam serving as the mainland hub of commercial activity.

The city offers a unique contrast: one side displays a laid-back lifestyle with colonial structures frozen in time, serene beaches, and backwaters, while the other side is a bustling modern landscape of ultramodern shopping complexes.

Heritage & Architecture

Hill Palace Museum

The largest archaeological museum in Kerala and former official residence of the Kochi Royal Family. Built in 1865, the complex features 49 buildings in traditional style surrounded by 52 acres of terraced land.

Bolgatty Palace

Built in 1744 by a Dutch trader on Bolgatty island, this is one of the oldest Dutch palaces outside Holland. Now a heritage hotel, it was once a property of the British Raj until 1947.

Mattancherry (Dutch) Palace

Built by the Portuguese in 1557 and later renovated by the Dutch. It is famous for its intricate carvings and murals depicting scenes from Hindu epics.

Jewish Synagogue

Constructed in 1568, this is one of the oldest synagogues in the world. Its highlight includes beautiful hand-painted floor tiles brought from China.

Iconic Landmarks

Chinese Fishing Nets

Introduced by traders from Kublai Khan's court, these exotic nets line the seafront. They use a unique indigenous system believed to be found only here outside China.

St. Francis Church

An ancient structure built by the Portuguese in 1503. It notably housed the grave of explorer Vasco Da Gama before his remains were returned to Portugal.

Backwater Cruises

Cochin is an enchanting backwater destination. Houseboat cruises offer exotic views of silvery beaches, swaying palms, and traditional coir making.

Nature & Recreation

Waterfalls

Visit Athirampally, where water plunges 80 feet, and the nearby Vazhachal waterfalls on the edge of the Sholayar forest range.

Veegaland (Wonderla)

A popular amusement park spread over green hills, featuring water theme rides, fast rides, and haunted houses for all ages.

Travel Guide

By Air

Cochin International Airport (Nedumbassery) is 25 km from the city, connecting major Indian and Middle Eastern cities.

By Rail

Three stations: Ernakulam Junction, Ernakulam Town, and Willingdon Island. Junction handles major northern trains.

Shopping

Head to M.G. Road for camel bone carvings, metal articles, and pine mats. GCDA complex is great for antiques and jewellery.

Don't Miss

  • Santa Cruz Basilica (Fort Kochi)
  • Jew Town Mattancherry
  • Backwater Houseboat Cruise
