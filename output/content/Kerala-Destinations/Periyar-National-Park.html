Periyar National Park

A Tiger Reserve and Elephant haven set high in the emerald Western Ghats.

Quick Facts

Location: Idukki District, Kerala
Area: 778 sq km
Altitude: 914m - 1830m
Best Time: October to June
Climate: Summer (21°C-31°C) | Winter (19°C-21°C)

History & Overview

Set high in the ranges of the Western Ghats, Periyar National Park and Tiger Reserve is the pride of Kerala. At the heart of the sanctuary lies a picturesque lake formed by the building of a dam in 1895.

This reservoir meanders around the contours of wooded hills, providing a permanent water source for local wildlife. While it is a designated Tiger Reserve, tourists primarily flock here to watch Indian elephants playing and bathing by the lake's edge.

Wildlife & Sightseeing

Majestic Mammals

Spot Indian Elephants, Gaur, Wild Pigs, Sambar, Barking Deer, and Mouse Deer. Rare sightings include the Indian Wild Dog (Dhole) and approximately 40 resident Tigers.

Primates & Rare Species

Home to four primate species: the rare Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Langur, Common Langur, and Bonnet Macaque. It is also a habitat for the elusive Nilgiri Tahr.

Birds & Reptiles

Birdlife includes Darters, Cormorants, Kingfishers, and the Malabar Hornbill. Reptile lovers may spot Monitor Lizards, Pythons, and occasionally King Cobras.

Lake Boat Cruises

The best way to explore the sanctuary. Cruises offer chances to see families of elephants and wild boar at the water's edge. Take the 7:00 AM boat for the best sighting chances.

Explore the Surroundings

Kumily (4 km)

A growing spice trade town equipped with excellent hotels and resorts. It is the main gateway for tourists visiting Periyar.

Cardamom Hills

Easily accessible from Periyar and Kumily via jeep or taxi trips. A beautiful landscape perfect for group excursions.

Travel Guide

By Air

Nearest airports are Kochi (200 km) and Madurai (140 km).

By Rail

Kottayam (114 km) is the nearest railhead connecting to major Indian cities.

By Road

Well-served by state and private buses from Kottayam, Ernakulam, and Madurai.

Travel Tip

Wildlife sightings are best when forest water sources dry up. Avoid heavy rain periods if tracking animals is your priority.
