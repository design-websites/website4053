Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

A lush haven on the banks of Vembanad Lake, home to indigenous gems and global travelers.

Quick Facts

Location: Kottayam District, Kerala
Area: 14 acres
Best Time: June & August (Local), Nov to Feb (Migratory)
Climate: Tropical (10°C - 26°C)

Overview

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary, also known as the Vembanad Bird Sanctuary, is a prominent destination for bird lovers located in the Kottayam district of Kerala. Situated alongside the beautiful Vembanad Lake, it provides an ideal habitat for both resident and migratory birds.

Interestingly, this charming area was originally a rubber plantation known as Baker's Estate. It was later transformed into a bird sanctuary by an Englishman, creating an unspoiled paradise for ornithologists.

Enriched Wildlife

Indigenous Species

  • Brahminy Kite & Egret
  • Heron & Waterfowl
  • Cormorant & Darter
  • Cuckoo, Owl & Moorhen

Migratory Guests

  • Siberian Crane
  • Flycatcher & Teal
  • Parrot, Lark & more

Many of these travelers arrive from as far as the Himalayan range.

How to Reach

By Air

The nearest airports are Kochi (76 km) and Thiruvananthapuram (160 km). International hubs include Chennai and Bangalore.

By Rail

Kottayam is the primary railhead, well-connected to major Kerala destinations.

By Road

Located just 12 km from Kottayam. Accessible via bus, taxi, or auto-rickshaw.

Visiting Guide

Travel Tip

To witness the majestic migratory species like the Siberian Crane, plan your visit between November and February.
