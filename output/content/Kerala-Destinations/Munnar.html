Munnar: The Green Paradise

A colonial-era hill retreat famous for its emerald tea plantations and misty peaks.

Quick Facts

Area: 557 sq kms
Altitude: 1,600 meters
Best Time: September to May
Rainfall: 270 cm (Annual)
Languages: Malayalam, Tamil, English
STD Code: 04865

History of Munnar

Munnar's history is deeply rooted in the colonial era. Discovered by Scottish planters, it became a favored retreat for the British to escape the heat of the plains. The Duke of Wellington was the first notable personality to visit this region.

The land was developed primarily for tea plantations. While written history dates back to the 10th century, prehistoric relics suggest the existence of a Stone Age civilization here. In 1895, the land was purchased by Messrs Finlay Muir & Company, eventually becoming part of Tata-Finlay Ltd. in 1976. Today, it is a hub for adventure pursuits including trekking, paragliding, and mountaineering.

Must-Visit Attractions

Anaimudi Peak

The highest peak in Southern India, rising to about 2700 meters. It is a feast for nature lovers, offering tranquil mountain atmosphere and ideal spots for camping and trekking.

Echo Point

Named after its natural echo phenomenon. It is said that hearing your echo call back three times is a sign of good luck.

Rajamala

Located 2700 meters above sea level, this is the natural habitat of the rare Nilgiri Tahr. It is a perfect spot for rejuvenation.

Kundala

Located 20km from Munnar, home to a picturesque artificial dam and a golf course owned by Tata Tea Ltd.

Scenic Water & Views

Power House & Kuthumkal Waterfalls

Power House falls from a height of 2000 meters over steep rocks, while Kuthumkal creates a mesmerizing foggy atmosphere from its spectacular heights.

Malankara Reservoir

Situated on the Thodupuzha-Moolavattom road, this reservoir is a popular spot for enthusiasts of boating and fishing.

Observatory Hills

Located at Nedumkandam, this spot offers exceptionally clear skies, making it a delight for spotting constellations and planets at night.

Travel Guide

By Air

Nearest airport is Kochi. Madurai and Coimbatore airports also serve the region with good connectivity.

By Rail

Munnar has no railway station. Use railheads at Kochi, Madurai, or Kottayam and complete the journey by taxi or bus.

By Road

Roads are well-built though winding. Regular state transport buses connect Munnar with neighboring cities.
