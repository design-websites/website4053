Alleppey: The Watery Paradise

A labyrinth of canals, backwaters, and beaches in the heart of God's Own Country.

Quick Facts

Area: 70.48 sq. km
Altitude: Sea level
Climate: Summer (22.5-35°C) | Winter (22-32°C)
Rainfall: 254 cm
Languages: Malayalam, English
STD Code: 0477

History & Heritage

Modern Alleppey was shaped significantly by Raja Kesava Das, the Diwan of Travancore, known as the "Maker of modern Alleppey." He transformed Alappuzha into a premier port town by constructing roads, canals, and warehouses.

Later developments under Velu Thampi Dalava brought the island of Pathiramanal under coconut cultivation and expanded paddy farming. The district also boasts the first post office and telegraph office in the erstwhile Travancore state.

Major Attractions

Alappuzha Beach

A popular picnic spot featuring a 137-year-old pier and an old lighthouse. The nearby Vijaya Beach Park offers boating and a children's park.

Kuttanad: The Rice Bowl

Famed for being the only place in the world where farming is done below sea level. It is a stunning 75 km stretch of emerald green paddy fields.

Pathiramanal Island

Accessible only by boat, this island in Vembanad Lake is a haven for rare migratory birds and a must-visit for bird lovers.

Krishnapuram Palace

A protected monument exhibiting Kerala architecture. It houses the Gajendra Moksham, the largest single band of mural painting in Kerala.

Culture & Traditions

Alappuzha's culture is inseparable from its legendary boat races and vibrant festivals. Onam, the harvest festival, is celebrated with immense brilliance, reflecting the warmth of the region.

Ambalapuzha Temple: Famous for its Kerala architecture and the traditional payasam (sweet pudding).
Arthunkal: Known for the St. Andrews Church and the annual feast of St. Sebastian in January.

Travel Guide

By Air

Cochin International Airport is the nearest. Visitors often take a taxi to Cochin and then a bus or train to Alleppey.

By Rail

Well-connected to major cities like Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai, New Delhi, and Kolkata via the Alappuzha station.

By Road

Frequent bus services connect Alleppey with Cochin, Thiruvananthapuram, Bangalore, and Mysore.

Excursions

  • Chavara Bhavan (Ancestral home shrine)
  • Edatgya Church (St. George's Church)
  • Sea View Park (Boating & Pool)
