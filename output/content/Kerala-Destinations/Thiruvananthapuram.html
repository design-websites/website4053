Thiruvananthapuram

The "Evergreen City of India" where royal shadows merge with modern horizons.

City Overview

Formerly known as Trivandrum, Thiruvananthapuram is the capital of Kerala. The city is named after the deity Sri Padmanabhaswamy and is mythologically believed to be the city of the huge serpent Anantha, upon which Lord Vishnu reclines.

Built upon seven hills on the sea coast, the city was nicknamed the "Evergreen City of India" by Mahatma Gandhi. Today, it is a bustling hub for Information Technology, contributing roughly 80% of the state's total software exports.

The Soul of the City

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

This architectural wonder, dedicated to Lord Vishnu, is a blend of Kerala and Tamil styles. It features musical pillars, a golden mandap, and a 500-pillared corridor adorned with sculptures and mural paintings.

* The temple was nominated as one of the 7 Wonders of the World.

Monuments & Museums

Napier Museum

A stunning architectural piece combining English, Saxon, and Travancore styles. It houses and preserves rare artifacts from the region's history.

Cultural Hotspots

The Sree Chithra Art Gallery, Zoological Park, and the galleries of Kanaka Kunnu Palace serve as the city's primary cultural centers.

Science & Space

Home to the Priyadarshini Space Planetarium (one of the largest in India) and the Kerala Science and Technology Museum.

Chachanehru Museum

An exclusive museum for children, located alongside other royal structures like Kuthiramalika Palace and Kowidar Palace.

Beaches & Recreation

The sapphire seas and gleaming sand beaches define the city's coastline.

Kovalam Beach

World-famous for its crescent coastline and water sports.

Shanghumukham

A hub for boating, sunsets, and evening strolls.

Veli Village

Tourist village offering horse riding and lush landscapes.

Visitor Info

Climate

Equatorial climate. Best time to visit: September to May.

Festivals

Don't miss the Swathi Thirunal Music Festival held annually at the Navarathri Mandapam.

Excursions

  • Padmanabhapuram Palace
  • Ponmudi Hill Station
  • Neyyar & Aruvikkara Dams
  • Peppara Wildlife Sanctuary
