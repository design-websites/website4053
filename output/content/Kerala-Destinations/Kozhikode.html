Kozhikode: Harbour of Honesty

A historic Malabar region where greenery, cool breezes, and ancient trade history converge.

City Overview

Kozhikode, also widely known as Calicut, has been a vital trade and commercial sector for centuries. Renowned for the integrity of its people, the city is affectionately called the "Harbour of Honesty" and sometimes the "Cock Fort."

It is a land of greenery and cool sea breezes, famous globally for its production and export of pepper, coconut, coffee, rubber, and lemon grass oil. The city is also the birthplace of iconic Indian personalities like V. K. Krishna Menon and P.T. Usha.

Best Time to Visit: September to February

Major Attractions

Mananchira Square

An architectural masterpiece built around the Mananchira tank. This green-carpeted area is surrounded by ethnic buildings, a musical fountain, and historic landmarks like the Public Library and Mishkal Mosque.

Kappad Beach

The historic shore where Vasco da Gama first landed in India on May 27, 1498. A stone monument stands today to commemorate this world-changing event.

Thali Temple

A revered Hindu shrine once famous for hosting 'Revathi Pattanam,' a legendary annual contest where artists showcased their skills before the Samuthiri rulers.

Dolphin Point

Located near Kozhikode Beach, this spot offers a pleasant view of dolphins playing in the sea water, perfect for early morning visitors.

Experience & Exploration

Regional Science Centre

Located at Jaffarkhan Colony, it features a planetarium and working models to explore the universe, alongside games and scientific gadgetry for children.

Thusharagiri Waterfall

A splendid natural fall known for its scenic beauty and tranquility, serving as a popular picnic spot for nature lovers.

Pisharikavu Temple

Home to some of the oldest murals in Kerala. The temple comes alive during its annual festival in April-May.

Quick Info
