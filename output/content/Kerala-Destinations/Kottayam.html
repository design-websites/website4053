Kottayam: The Gateway to Backwaters

A city of contrasts where busy commerce meets the shimmering silence of lush greenery.

Quick Facts

Area: 2203 Sq.Km
Altitude: Sea level
Best Time: August to March
Languages: Malayalam, Tamil, Hindi, English
Summer: 22.5°C - 35°C
STD Code: 0481

History & Overview

Kottayam is an enchanting city situated south of Cochin. It is a place of many contrasts: one part is a bustling commercial center, while the other offers shimmering backwaters and lush greenery for repose and relaxation.

The city is renowned for its beautiful churches, some dating back nearly 700 years, featuring intricate carvings and paintings. The most famous is St. Mary's Church, which houses the remains of Alphonsa and Father Chavara.

Major Attractions

Vembanad Lake

Located 12 km west of the city, this is the largest freshwater lake in Asia. Its vast shores and islands support exotic flora and fauna, making Kottayam a premier backwater destination.

Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary

An ornithologist's dream set in the marshy area of Vembanad Lake. It is a breeding ground for resident and migratory birds like the Siberian stork, darter, and egret.

Vaikom Temple

An important Hindu pilgrimage center known for its unique architectural style. Devotees gather here throughout the day to perform holy rituals.

Thazhathangadi Mosque

Believed to be over 1,000 years old, this mosque was built by Malik Dinar. It is a historic pilgrimage site admired for its beautiful architecture.

Things to Do

Backwater Cruises

Experience nature's splendor on a luxury houseboat across the shimmering waters of Vembanad Lake. These cruises offer a window into exotic locations and traditional life along the Kerala backwaters, ensuring a memorable stay.

Historic Churches

Valiyapalli Church

Built in the 16th century by Jerusalem migrants, it is famous for its Persian crosses—two huge granite structures brought from Cranganmore.

Travel Guide

By Air

Cochin is the nearest airport. Regular flights connect Cochin with Delhi and other major hubs.

By Rail

Kottayam railway station (2 km from bus stand) links to major Kerala cities and national hubs via trains like the Kerala Express.

By Road

Two bus stands near the city center offer KSRTC and interstate services to neighboring states.

By Ferry

The ferry stand (3 km from railway station) connects to Alleppey, Munnar, and Ambalapuzha.
