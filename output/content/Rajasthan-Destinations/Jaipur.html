Jaipur: The Pink City

India's first planned city and the magnificent capital of the Maharajahs.

Historical Legacy

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is the first planned city of India. Known worldwide as the Pink City, it forms a vital part of the Golden Triangle tourist circuit along with Delhi and Agra.

Area: 200.4 sq. km
Best Season: Oct to mid-March
Hindi, Rajasthani, English
STD Code: 0141

Major Sightseeing

Amber Fort

A 16th-century masterpiece of Hindu and Mughal architecture, famous for the ravishing Sheesh Mahal (Mirror Palace).

Hawa Mahal

The 1799 "Palace of Winds" featuring 953 screened windows designed for royal ladies to watch street festivals.

Jantar Mantar

The world's largest stone observatory, built to measure local time, altitude, and eclipses with precision.

Jal Mahal

A red sandstone palace in the middle of Man Sagar Lake, where four floors remain submerged under water.

Vibrant Festivals

Elephant Festival

Elephants participate in polo, races, and processions groomed in gold.

Kite Festival

Celebrated on Jan 14, where thousands of kites cover the sky in fierce competition.

Travel Logistics

By Air

Jaipur Airport (Sanganer) is 13 km away. It is the only international airport in Rajasthan.

By Rail

The station in Bani Park connects to major cities. The "Palace on Wheels" also stops here.

Climate

Summer: 45°C Max | Winter: 22°C Max to 8°C Min (evenings can drop to 4°C).
