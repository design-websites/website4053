Sariska National Park

A majestic tiger reserve amidst the rugged Aravalli hills of Alwar.

Wilderness of the Aravallis

Located just 37 kms from Alwar, Sariska National Park is dominated by sharp cliffs and narrow valleys of the Aravalli hills. Established in 1955 and brought under Project Tiger in 1979, this 765.80 sq km reserve is a premier destination for witnessing the majestic Royal Bengal Tiger.

Major Species: Tiger, Leopard, Wild Dog
Best Season: November to March
Location: Alwar, Rajasthan
Terrain: Dry Deciduous & Bamboo

Wildlife & Avian Treasures

Prominent Mammals

Home to Chital, Sambhar, Nilgai, Wild Boar, and carnivores like the Leopard, Hyena, Jackal, and Jungle Cat.

Avian World

A paradise for birdwatchers featuring the Crested Serpent Eagle, Great Indian Horned Owl, Peafowl, and Golden Backed Woodpecker.

Historical Landmarks

Sariska Palace

Built in 1892 as a hunting lodge by the Maharaja of Alwar, now a heritage hotel within the sanctuary.

Kankwari Fort

A 10th-century Mughal structure where Aurangzeb once imprisoned his brother Dara Shikoh.

Ancient Temples

Explore the 6th-13th century Neelkanth Shiva temples and the Budha Hanumab Temple near Pandupol.

Taalvriksh

Hot and cold springs known for their medicinal value located near the park.

Adventure Safaris

Explore the park’s rugged beauty via Jeep Safaris or on elephant back. Safaris offer spectacular opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography in the heart of the reserve.

How to Reach

By Air

Jaipur International Airport is the nearest, located 115 km away.

By Rail

Alwar (36 km away) is the nearest major railhead, well-connected to Delhi and Jaipur.

By Road

Sariska is 200 km from Delhi (5 hrs drive) and 107 km from Jaipur.

Climate

Winter: 10°C - 25°C
Summer: Up to 45°C
