Bikaner: The Royal Desert

A majestic staging post of the ancient Silk Route in the heart of the Thar.

A Legacy of Red Sandstone

Founded in 1486 A.D. by Rao Bikaji, Bikaner is a royal city encircled by a mammoth fortification wall over 5 km in circumference. Its unique red sandstone architecture and sprawling sand dunes make it a sanctuary for nature and history lovers alike.

Rajasthan, India
Best Season: October to March
Hindi, Urdu, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0145

Top Attractions

Camel Breeding Farm

The only facility of its kind in all of Asia. It highlights Bikaner’s historical reliance on camel caravans as a major staging post of the Silk Route.

Junagarh Fort

A primary landmark and central shopping hub. The fort area is famous for its intricate architecture and vivacious murals.

Vibrant Fairs & Festivals

Camel Festival

Held every January during the full moon. Features camel races, dances, and a colorful environment typical of Rajasthan.

Karni Mata Fair

Held twice a year (Mar/Apr and Sept/Oct) at the desert citadel of Deshnok in honor of the goddess Karni Mata.

Kite Festival

Held in April/May (Teej Festival) to commemorate the day the foundation of the city was laid.

Kapil Muni Fair

A November gathering at Kolayat Lake involving a holy dip and a renowned cattle fair.

Traveler's Guide

By Air

Nal Airport (17km) handles civilian flights Nov-Mar. Nearest International: Jaipur/Jodhpur.

By Rail

Well-connected to Delhi (12 hours), Mumbai, and Kolkata. Station is in the city heart.

What to Wear

Loose cottons for summer; light woolens for winter. Wear comfortable shoes for walking.

Climate

Summer: 28°C - 41.8°C

Winter: 5°C - 23.2°C
