Bhiwadi: The Industrial Hub

Rajasthan's economic engine and a key part of the National Capital Region (NCR).

Strategic Gateway

Bhiwadi is an economically vital industrial town in the Alwar district. Its proximity to Delhi places it within the National Capital Region (NCR), making it a magnet for global industrialists and a primary driver for Rajasthan's economic growth.

A Global Industrial Center

Recognized as the 3rd largest industrial center in India, Bhiwadi hosts thousands of units ranging from steel and electronics to pharmaceuticals and textiles. All major industries are represented by the Bhiwadi Manufacturing Association (BMA).

Key MNCs Present

  • Honda Siel & Hero Honda
  • Pepsi & United Breweries
  • Gillette & Ray Ban
  • Saint Gobain & Kajaria Ceramics

Economic Stats

Over ₹30,000 crores in MOU investments have been signed by the government to further enhance industrial capacity and infrastructure.

Attractions & Surroundings

While primarily a commercial sub-region, several historic landmarks lie in close proximity to the town.

  • Vijay Mandir Palace

    Constructed in 1918 by Maharaja Jai Singh, featuring a stunning garden and a rippling lake.

  • Lakes of Alwar

    The nearby Siliserh and Jaisamand Lakes offer beautiful retreats built by the former rulers of Alwar.

Location & Climate

Connectivity

  • Gurgaon: 40 km
  • Delhi: 75 km
  • Alwar: 90 km
  • Jaipur: 200 km

Climate

Summer: Up to 48°C (May-June)

Winter: Low of 2°C (Dec-Jan)

Why Invest?

Low property rates, low pollution density, and modernized townships attracting hard-earned investment.
