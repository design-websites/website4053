Pushkar: The Sacred Town

A divine pilgrimage site nestled in the heart of Rajasthan's Aravalli hills.

Divine Significance

Pushkar is one of India's most revered pilgrimage destinations. Known for its spiritual energy and ancient temples, the town centers around the holy lake, drawing devotees and travelers from across the globe.

Rajasthan, North West India
Best Season: September to February
Hindi, English, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0145

Major Sightseeing

Pushkar Lake

The heart of the town, surrounded by 52 bathing ghats where pilgrims take sacred dips.

Brahma Temple

One of the very few temples in the world dedicated to Lord Brahma, the creator.

Savitri Temple

Located on a hilltop, offering a breathtaking panoramic view of the entire Pushkar town.

Pushkar Fair

The annual livestock and cultural fair that transforms the desert into a kaleidoscope of colors.

Climate & Best Time

The ideal time to visit Pushkar is between September and February when the weather is pleasant.

Note: Summers in Rajasthan are generally hot, making winter the preferred season for exploration.

Travel Logistics

By Air

Jaipur International Airport (140 km) is the nearest airport to Pushkar.

By Rail

Ajmer Junction (13 km away) is the nearest railway station.

By Road

Excellent connectivity from Jaipur, Ajmer, Delhi, Ahmedabad, and Mount Abu.

Distances

  • Ajmer: 13 Km
  • Jaipur: 140 Km
