Ajmer: The Heart of Rajasthan

A spiritual sanctuary and historic stronghold founded in the 7th century.

Historical Legacy

Named after its founder, Raja Ajai Pal Chauhan (7th century), Ajmer was the seat of the Chauhan dynasty until its fall to Mohammed Ghauri. Today, it stands as a unique cultural melting pot and a premier pilgrimage destination for both Hindus and Muslims.

  • Significance: Famous for Dargah of Sufi Saint Chisti
  • Languages: Hindi, Urdu, Rajasthani
  • Best Season: October to March
  • STD Code: 0145

Prime Attractions

Dargah Sharif

The sacred tomb of Sufi saint Khwaja Moin-ud-din Chisti. Revered by Mughal rulers, the structure features a gold-plated dome and architectural touches ranging from Humayun to Shah Jahan.

Adhai-Din-Ka-Jhopraa

A masterpiece of Indo-Islamic architecture built in just two and a half days. It is celebrated for its front screen wall with seven pointed arches and distinctive carved pillars.

Taragarh Fort

This colossal fort watches over the city from the Aravalli ranges. It houses the Miran-Saheb-ki-Dargah and offers stunning panoramic views of Ajmer.

Ana Sagar Lake

An artificial lake named after Anaji Chauhan. The favorite local outing spot features marble pavilions (Baradari) built by Shah Jahan.

Fairs & Shopping

Urs Fair

Held during the first six days of Rajab to commemorate the Sufi saint. It is a period of great reverence attracting pilgrims of all communities.

Shopping

Famous for traditional silver jewellery, antiques, tie-and-dye fabrics, and embroidered 'jutis'.

Travel Guide

By Air

The nearest airport is located in Jaipur.

By Train

Well connected to Delhi via Shatabdi Express, Pink City Express, and Chetak Express.

Distances

  • Jaipur: 131 Km
  • New Delhi: 389 Km
  • Pushkar: 11 Km

Climate

Summers can reach 45ºC (April-June). Light cottons are recommended.
