Bhilwara: The Land of Courage

A historic Mewar trading center with a legacy of sacrifice and resilience.

Explore Bhilwara

Established 300 to 400 years ago, Bhilwara was destroyed many times due to ruler confrontations before finally flourishing in the 18th Century. Today, it is recognized as a major Mewar Acchal Trading centre and the "Land of Courage and Sacrifice."

Key Attractions

Mejha Dam

The city's largest dam and a primary water reservoir. It features a rest house, swimming pool, and children's swings, serving as a vibrant picnic spot during the monsoon.

Harni Mahadev

A revered Shivling located under a large stone near a waterfall. The nearby Chamunda Mata temple offers a panoramic view of the entire city.

Menal

Home to the Mahanaleshwar Temple built by Chauhan rulers and a stunning 150-foot waterfall that crashes into a V-shaped valley.

Joganiya Mata

An 8th-century temple in a dense forest. Once a site for animal sacrifices, it was reformed by Jain Sadhvi Yaskanwarji and now features modern facilities for visitors.

Heritage Landmarks

  • Triveni Confluence

    The meeting point of the Menali, Bards, and Badach rivers. It is a holy site where pilgrims gather for ritual dips during auspicious months like Kartik and Magh.

  • Mangalgarh Fort

    A naturally secured fortress standing 1850 feet above sea level, housing numerous temples of gods and goddesses along its 1 km length.

  • Shahpura Art & Freedom

    Famous for 700-year-old traditional paintings and award-winning artists. It was a center for the Ramsnehi Sampraday and the home of the freedom-fighting Barhat family.

  • Bijolian Movement

    Historically significant for the Kissan (farmer) movement supported by Gandhiji, where farmers successfully fought for their rights against landlords.
