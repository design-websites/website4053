Bikaner & Bharatpur

Royal desert cities and staging posts of the ancient Silk Route.

Desert Majesty

Located in the heart of the Thar Desert, Bikaner was founded in 1486 A.D. by Rao Bikaji. This royal city is surrounded by a massive fortification wall over 5 km in circumference. Historically, it served as a significant staging post on the Silk Route for camel caravans.

Rajasthan, India
Best Season: October to March
Hindi, Urdu, Rajasthani
STD Code: 0145

Climate & Environment

Bikaner features a typical desert climate with scorching days and chilly nights. The rocky soil and sand dunes provide a captivating backdrop for nature lovers.

  • Summer (April - June): 28°C to 41.8°C
  • Winter (Nov - Feb): 5°C to 23.2°C

Vivacious Fairs & Festivals

Camel Festival

Held every January during the full moon. Features camel races, camel dances, and vibrant rides celebrating the "Ship of the Desert."

Gangaur Fair

Celebrated in March/April in honor of Goddess Gauri. Women perform rituals and processions for marital bliss.

Kite (Teej) Festival

Held in April/May to commemorate the day the foundation of Bikaner was laid.

Karni Mata Fair

Held twice a year during Navratra (March/April and Sept/Oct) at the desert citadel of Deshnok.

Shopping & Wares

Bikaner is a hub for artistic skills and traditional crafts. Key areas include Kote Gate and Junagarh Fort.

  • Camel Hide Goods (Belts, Bags, Jootis)
  • Camel & Sheep Wool Carpets
  • Kundan Jewellery & Uncut Stones
  • Nokha Quilts & Sanganeri Prints

Travel Logistics

By Air

Nal Airport (17 km) serves civilian flights Nov-March. Nearest International: Jodhpur & Jaipur.

By Rail

Station in the city heart. Well connected to Delhi (12 hours), Mumbai, and Kolkata.

By Road

Intercity bus station near Lalgarh Palace. Luxury coaches link to Delhi, Udaipur, and Agra.

What to Wear

Loose cottons in summer; light woolens in winter. Use hats/scarves and sunscreen.
