Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary

A lush stretch of evergreen forests providing sanctuary to Goa's rare wildlife.

Sanctuary Profile

Area: 240 square kilometers
Best Time: October to March
Location: Mollem, Goa (65 km from Panaji)
Major Attractions: Panther, Gaur, Dudhsagar Falls

History & Vegetation

While Goa is famous for its beaches, it is also home to incredible biodiversity. Formerly known as Mollem Game Sanctuary, this protected area was renamed Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary to preserve its 240 sq. km of pristine wilderness.

The sanctuary consists of tropical evergreen and moist deciduous forests. Major tree species include Xylia, Lagerstroemia, and Terminalia. The landscape is characterized by thick grass and primeval jungle environments.

Wildlife & Birdwatching

Animal Kingdom

Spot leopards, panthers, mouse deer, barking deer, wild boar, sambhar, Malabar giant squirrels, and the iconic Gaur (Indian Bison). Rare sightings include pangolins and flying squirrels.

Aviary Paradise

Home to approximately 200 bird species. Look for the Indian hornbill, fairy bluebird, black woodpecker, wagtails, kingfishers, and emerald doves from strategically located watchtowers.

Must-See Landmarks

Dudhsagar Falls

The "Ocean of Milk" is Goa's tallest waterfall at 310m (India's 5th tallest). It features a spectacular South Central Railway aqueduct passing through it. Access is via hiking or 4-wheel drive from Collem.

Tambdi Surla Temple

An exquisite 12th-century Shiva temple built of basalt. It features a garbhagriha, antarala, and intricate carvings of elephants and chains on its pillars.

Devil's Canyon

An eerie canyon of water-carved crevices downstream from Dudhsagar, created from solid rock by serpentine underwater currents.

Visitor Info

Hours

8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Everyday

Entry Fees

Adults: Rs. 20

Children: Rs. 5

Transport

By Air: Dabolim Airport (80 km)

By Rail: Colem Station (6 km)

By Road: State buses & taxis available.

Safety Note

Access to Dudhsagar is dangerous and restricted during the monsoons (June-July). Hiring a local guide for Tambdi Falls is required.
