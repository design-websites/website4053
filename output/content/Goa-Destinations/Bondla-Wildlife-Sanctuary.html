Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary

Explore Goa's family-friendly wilderness nestled in the Western Ghats.

Sanctuary Snapshot

Area: 8 square kilometers
Location: Ponda Taluka, Central Goa
Best Time: October to March
Timings: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Closed Thursdays)

About Bondla

Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary is located 52 km from Panaji in the foothills of the Western Ghats. While it is considered "rather small" by official standards, it is a popular destination featuring a Mini Zoo, Deer Safari Park, and Botanical Gardens.

The sanctuary is home to moist deciduous forests and small patches of evergreens. It is one of the best places to see Goa's state animal, the Gaur, along with the state tree, the Terminalia Crenulata (Matti).

Major Attractions

Zoological Park

A tiny zoo set in natural surroundings featuring elephants, panthers, lions, sloth bears, and various reptiles.

Deer Safari Park

Take a vehicle through the park to move among sambar and spotted deer in their natural habitat.

Formal & Botanical Gardens

Lush lawns featuring exotic trees, colorful roses, and flowering plants located right in the middle of the forest.

Nature Education Centre

An interpretive center with exhibits, an attached library, and video movies screened upon request.

Wildlife & Nature Trails

Birdwatching

Bondla is home to over a hundred bird species. Birdwatchers can contact the Park Range Office for the best spotting locations.

Animal Sightings

Use the watchtowers to spy on gaur, sambar, and wild boar at water-holes. You may also encounter jungle cats, porcupines, or ant eaters.

Archaeological Exhibits

Discover ancient relief motifs of the deity Gajalaxmi dating from the sixth to eighth centuries.

How to Reach

By Road

Located off NH 4A. Branch off at Mollem (20 km) or Tiska (14 km). Panjim is 52 km away.

By Rail

Nearest stations include Collem (28 km) on the Hubli line or Carambolim (40 km) on the Konkan Railway.

By Air

Dabolim Airport is approximately 70 km away via Ponda and Usgao.

Visitor Tip

A bus service operates from Tiska to Bondla every day except Thursday. Elephant rides are generally offered in the afternoons around 4:00 PM.
