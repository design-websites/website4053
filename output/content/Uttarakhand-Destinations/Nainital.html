Nainital Tour

City of Lakes - Nestled in the Kumaon region of the Central Himalayas.

The Naini Legend

Nainital is famous for the Naini Lake, a blue-green body of water surrounded by hills on all sides. Named after Goddess Naini, the town is a photographer's delight and offers everything from boating to soft treks and golfing.

Snow View

Accessible via cable car, it offers a glittering panorama of Himalayan snows.

Naina Peak

The highest point (8,568 ft), providing a bird's-eye view of Nainital.

Naina Devi Temple

A sacred worship place for Hindus, located where the eyes of Sati are said to have fallen.

High Altitude Zoo

Home to tigers, bears, and deer in a beautiful high-altitude garden.

Travel Logistics

Rail: Kathgodam (34 km away) is the nearest station.
Road: Well-connected buses from Delhi (approx. 8 hours).
