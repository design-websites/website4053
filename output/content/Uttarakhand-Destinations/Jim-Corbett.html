Jim Corbett National Park

India's First Tiger Reserve - A Paradise for Wildlife Lovers.

A Legacy of Conservation

Lying in the Nainital and Pauri Garhwal districts, Jim Corbett National Park is the oldest national park in India. In 1973, it became the site for the launching of Project Tiger. The park features mountainous terrain in the Shivalik and Outer Himalaya regions.

Flora and Fauna

The park boasts 600 species of birds and a rich variety of trees like Sal, Khair, and Sissoo. It is home to the rare Royal Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, and three species of otter. Visitors can explore the jungle through Elephant, Jeep, or Canter Safaris.

Wildlife Highlights

  • ● Asian Elephant
  • ● Sambar & Barking Deer
  • ● King Cobra & Gharial
