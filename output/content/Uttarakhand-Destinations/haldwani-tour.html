Haldwani Tour

Gateway to Kumaon - A scenic destination nestled in the Nainital district.

Location and History

Haldwani, known as the ‘gateway of Kumaon,’ is the third largest city in Uttarakhand by population. Located in the Nainital district along with its twin city Kathgodam, it was established in the 19th century and has since become a major administrative and trading hub.

Places of Interest

Haldwani offers a variety of attractions for nature lovers and spiritual seekers alike:

  • Gaula Barrage: A beautiful picnic spot on the banks of the river Gaula, perfect for trekking and camping.
  • Hedakhan Ashram: A peaceful retreat famous for its spiritual tranquility and scenic river views.
  • Sat Tal: Located 22 km away, this unique fusion of seven freshwater lakes is surrounded by dense oak and pine forests.
  • Jim Corbett National Park: India's oldest national park, situated 55 km from Haldwani, hosting nearly 448 species of flora and fauna.

Travel Tips

  • ● Visit the local markets, among the largest grain and fruit markets in India.
  • ● Explore Sanjay Van for bird watching and spotting rare migratory birds.
  • ● Attend the Uttaryani fair at Chitrashila Ghat during Makar Sankranti.
