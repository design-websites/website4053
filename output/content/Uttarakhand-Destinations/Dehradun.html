Dehradun Tourism

Capital of Uttarakhand - Nestled in the Doon Valley between the Yamuna and Ganges.

Overview & History

Dehradun, properly termed as Dehra Doon, serves as the capital city of Uttarakhand. The name comes from 'Dehra' meaning camp and 'Doon' meaning river valley. According to the Mahabharata, the legendary teacher Dronacharya lived here, leading to its nickname, "Drona-nagri."

Important Places to Visit

  • Clock Tower: Also known as Ghanta-ghar, a historical structure with six working clocks.
  • Military Institutes: Home to the Indian Military Academy (IMA) and Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC).
  • Moti Bazar and Paltan Bazar: Famous local markets for shopping and traditional bakery products.
  • Scientific Hub: Host to the ONGC headquarters and Forest Survey of India.

Climate & Logistics

Enjoy a cool climate due to the Sivalik Hills. The city is well-connected by rail and the Jolly Grant Airport (25 km away).
