Rishikesh Tour

The World Capital of Yoga - Where spirituality meets high-octane adventure.

Spiritual Hub & Gateway to Himalayas

Located on the banks of the Ganges, Rishikesh is the nickname "the world capital of Yoga." It offers a tranquil environment for those seeking peace, alongside thrilling opportunities for adventure sports like White Water Rafting, Cliff Jumping, and Kayaking.

Landmarks and Temples

  • Lakshman Jhula: An iron suspension bridge built in 1939, offering spectacular views of the entire city.
  • Bharat Mandir: The oldest temple in the town, built in the 12th century by Adiguru Shankracharya.
  • Triveni Ghat: The main ghat where pilgrims take a holy dip and witness the evening Ganga Aarti.

Activities

  • ● White Water Rafting
  • ● Yoga & Meditation Retreats
  • ● Rock Climbing & Trekking
