Lakshadweep Islands

India's only coral islands, serenely set in the Arabian Sea.

Island Paradise

Lakshadweep is a group of 36 islands in the Arabian Sea and is the smallest Union Territory of India. These are India's only coral islands, though only 10 are inhabited due to inadequate drinking water.

Major Tourist Islands

Kavaratti

The capital features white sandy beaches, spectacular woodcarvings in the Ujira mosque, and a marine aquarium.

Minicoy

The second largest island, known for its crescent shape, Tuna fishing, and iconic lighthouse.

Agatti

Offers soul-stirring views of coral reefs and lagoons filled with multicolored fish.

Kalpeni

Ideal for swimming and reef-walks, with landscape beauty that is unmatched.

Travel Tips

Best Time: October to May.

Monsoons: Ship-based tourism is shunned; helicopter services are used instead.
