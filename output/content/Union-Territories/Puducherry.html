Puducherry

A serene escape on the Coromandel Coast with French heritage.

The French Connection

Puducherry is a small, serene city on the Coromandel Coast, established by the French. It uniquely includes enclaves from three different states: Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Andhra Pradesh.

Spirituality & Culture

Shri Aurobindo Ashram

Founded by Sri Aurobindo, it is a center for yoga and features a cottage industry for handmade paper.

Auroville (City of Dawn)

An international township founded in 1968, representing global understanding through unique architecture.

Pristine Beaches

Paradise Beach

Also known as Plage Paradiso, it is perfect for beach sports and leisurely holidays.

Serenity Beach

A 1.5 km long beach suitable for relaxation and swimming, once a site of colonial battles.

Must See

  • Botanical Gardens: Designed in formal French style with 1500 tree varieties.
  • Manakula Vinayagar: A 500-year-old temple with 44 different Vinayaka poses.
  • Government Square: Houses the Legislative Assembly and beautiful organizzated gardens.
