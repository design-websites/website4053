Dadra & Nagar Haveli

A sylvan paradise of winding rivers and lush green forests.

Nature's Bounty

Gifted with nature's bounty, this territory is a land of spell-binding beauty with winding rivers, unimaginable waterfronts, and distant mountain ranges. It is a heaven for those seeking a tranquil holiday amidst quaint sylvan surroundings.

Iconic Gardens & Waterfronts

Vanganga Lake & Island Garden

Features Japanese-style bridges, thatched huts, and paddleboats. Over 40 Hindi film hit songs have been filmed here.

Hirwa Van Garden

Known for roaring waterfalls, misty cascades, and machan-style cottages amidst a forest of flowers.

Dudhni Waterfront

Provides breathtaking views of the water spread over Madhuban Dam with luxurious bankside tents.

Khanvel

Situated 20 km from Silvassa, this serene landscape features rolling meadows and native-styled cottages.

The Tribal Cultural Museum

Dedicated to preserving the vanishing tribal culture, this museum houses an extensive collection of masks, musical apparatus, hunting tools, and life-size statues of local tribes.

Festivals

  • ● Tarpa Festival: Annual cultural celebration.
  • ● Diwaso: Celebrated by Dhodia and Varli tribes.
  • ● Kite Festival: Organized to encourage tourism.
