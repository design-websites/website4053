Andaman & Nicobar

Explore 572 bright emerald islands and pristine coral reefs in the Bay of Bengal.

Destination Overview

Andaman and Nicobar is a lovely place to visit with around 500 islands spreading across a total distance of 780 Kms. Port Blair is the capital of this Union Territory situated in the Bay of Bengal. The islands are renowned for their pristine clean coral beaches and lush flora.

Key Islands & Attractions

Port Blair City

Visit the historic Cellular Jail, Fisheries Museum, Samudrika-Marine Museum, and Anthropological Museum.

Viper Island

Just 30 minutes from the city, featuring the historic open-space jail known as the Women jail.

Havelock Island

A world-famous beach known for its golden sand and striking bluish sea water.

Ross Island

Once a British colony, this island is just a 30-minute sea journey from Port Blair.

Unique Natural Wonders

Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park is famous for its underwater corals and diverse marine life. Madhuban serves as a training ground for elephants and houses unusual birds and butterflies. For those seeking adventure, Baratang offers a journey through thick forests to see limestone caves and mud volcanoes.

Quick Travel Guide

  • Chirya Tapoo: Known as Bird Island with a dedicated sanctuary.
  • Mount Harriet: Highest hill in South Andaman (365m) for excellent city views.
  • Chatam Island: Home to perhaps the largest Saw Mill in Asia.
  • Katchal Island: Famous for its rubber plantations and scheduled tribe population.
