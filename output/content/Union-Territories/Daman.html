Daman and Diu

Coastal enclaves of historic forts and golden beaches.

The Coastal Enclave

Daman & Diu is the second smallest Union Territory of India, located on the west coast and spanning 122 square km. September to April is considered the best time to visit.

Sightseeing in Daman

Moti Daman Fort

A colossal polygonal fort housing major official buildings like the Secretariat.

Kadaiya Lake Garden

A famous man-made destination featuring islands connected by bridges and playful toy trains.

Sightseeing in Diu

Nagoa Beach

The most popular semi-circular beach, perfect for water sports and playful waves.

Gangeshwar Temple

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, featuring five lingas rinsed by sea waves on the shore.

Travel Info

Official Language: Influenced by Gujarati.

Climate: Summers range from 17° to 38°C.
