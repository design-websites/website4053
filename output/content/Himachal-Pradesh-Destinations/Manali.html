Manali: Valley of the Gods

The Switzerland of India, where mythology meets breathtaking Himalayan landscapes.

Traveler's Snapshot

Altitude: 1,926 - 2,050 metres
Best Time: April to July & Sept to Nov
Summer: Max 25°C, Min 12°C
Winter: Max 14.5°C, Min 2°C
Languages: Pahari, Hindi, English
STD Code: 01902

Legend & History

Manali literally means the 'Home of Manu'. According to Hindu mythology, Manu was the lawgiver who survived a great flood and found refuge here to recreate human life.

Once known as the "end of the habitable world," Manali is situated at the head of the Kullu valley. It is a land of fruit-laden orchards, picturesque hamlets, and mountains shrouded by silent snows.

Major Attractions

Hadimba Temple

Built in 1533 A.D., this four-tiered pagoda-shaped temple is dedicated to Goddess Hadimba and is set within a dense wooden forest of deodar.

Rohtang Pass (3979 m)

The gateway to Lahaul, Spiti, and Leh. It offers spectacular mountain views and is open from June to October.

Solang Valley

Located 13 km from Manali, it offers splendid views of glaciers and snow-capped peaks. Famous for ski slopes and winter festivals.

Vashist Hot Springs

Renowned for natural hot sulphur springs with separate bathing tanks, accompanied by pyramidal stone temples dedicated to Vashist Muni and Lord Ram.

Adventure & Culture

Adventure Sports

Manali is a hub for skiing, hiking, mountaineering, paragliding, rafting, kayaking, and mountain biking. The Mountaineering Institute (3 km from town) provides specialized training.

Tibetan Monasteries

Visit newly constructed colorful monasteries in the town and Aleo to buy authentic carpets and Tibetan handicrafts.

Kothi & Rahala Falls

Kothi is a picturesque village on the road to Rohtang, a favorite for poets and filmmakers. Nearby, the Rahala Water Fall (16 km away) is a fascinating picnic spot.

Hidden Gems

  • Nehary Kund: A natural spring of cold clear water where Nehru used to drink during his stays.
  • Arjun Gufa: A cave near Prini village where Arjun is said to have meditated.
  • Jagatsukh: Famous for Shikhara style temples dedicated to Lord Shiva and Sandhya Gayatri.
  • Club House: Located on the bank of Manalsu Nallah, offering indoor games and picnic spots.

How to Reach

By Air

Bhuntar Airport is 52 km south of Manali. Flights connect to Delhi via Shimla.

By Rail

Narrow-gauge: Jogindernagar (135 km). Broad-gauge: Chandigarh (310 km) and Pathankot (355 km).

By Road

Connected to Delhi (585 km via Mandi), Shimla (270 km), Chandigarh, and Leh (July-Oct).
