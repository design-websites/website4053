Chamba Tour

The ancient capital of Pahari Kings, nestled on the banks of the River Ravi.

Historical Overview

Situated in Himachal Pradesh on the banks of the river Ravi, Chamba was once the capital of the Pahari Kings. The city was named after Princess Champavati by her father, King Sahil Verma.

Chamba is celebrated for its exquisite handicrafts and ancient temples. The city has preserved its social customs and traditional conditions, and is still home to old tribes such as the Gaddies and Pangwalas.

Ancient Shrines

The temples of Chamba are richly carved and bear a resemblance to those found in Rajasthan. A unique feature of Chamba's spiritual landscape is a row of three Vishnu and three Shiva temples near the palace, all featuring distinct shikaras or spires.

The Laxmi Narayan Temple is the oldest and most richly carved shrine in the city.

Major Attractions

Chaughan

A large grassy ground at the city center used for trading and public functions. It hosts the annual Minjar Procession, a major cultural and sporting event.

Bhuri Singh Museum

Houses Chamba's cultural heritage, including miniature paintings of the Kangra and Basholi schools and murals saved from the Rang Mahal Palace.

Akhand Chandi Palace

Recognizable by its distinct green roof, this former royal residence now serves as a Government College and District Library.

Bharmaur

The ancient capital of Chamba, surrounded by thick forests and home to the Lakshmi Devi, Narsingh, and Ganesh temples.

Natural Regions

Pangi (Chenab Valley)

A cold and desolate region where the Chandrabhaga river flows through rugged hills. It is bounded by the Great Himalayas and the Pir Panjal ranges.

Wilderness Areas

Explore Sarol's picnic spots and sheep breeding farms, or the deep gorges of the Chenab River at Killar and the historical rest houses at Purthi.

Travel Snapshot

Wildlife

Higher regions: Snow leopard, marmot, and ibex. Lower regions: Musk deer, wild pigs, and brown bear.

Saho

Home to the Chandrashekhar Temple dedicated to Lord Shiva on the banks of the Sal River.

Events

Visit during the Minjar Procession at Chaughan to witness vibrant cultural activities and sports.
