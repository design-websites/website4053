Dalhousie Tour

Experience the secluded charm and colonial elegance of Himachal's premier hill station.

Hill Station Profile

Area: 13 sq km
Altitude: 6678 feet above sea level
Best Time: Throughout the year
STD Code: (91) - 1899

History of Dalhousie

Dalhousie was once the dream destination of the former British Governor General, Lord Dalhousie. What sets it apart from counterparts like Shimla and Mussoorie is its secluded charm and laid-back lifestyle.

The hill station offers a placid atmosphere with a wonderful backdrop of snow-laden mountain peaks. Travelers can explore a host of colonial structures and escape the hum-drum of city life in this serene Himachal destination.

Major Sightseeing

Subhash Baodi

A popular picnic spot situated at 2085 mts. It offers picturesque environs, a pleasant climate, and commanding views of distant snow-covered peaks.

Kalatope

Located at 2438 mts, this attraction features a beautiful palace, forest rest house, and a wildlife sanctuary home to ibex, deer, bears, and leopards.

Satdhara

Known as the "Seven Streams," these springs are believed to contain medicinal properties for curing chronic diseases. It is located on the way to Panjpulla.

St. Andrew's Church

Built in 1903 in Scottish architectural style, this church is also known as the Church of Scotland. It remains in excellent condition near the bus stand.

Popular Getaway: Khajjiar

Situated 27 km from Dalhousie, Khajjiar is a quaint hamlet in the Himalayan foothills. Originally developed as a British summer resort, it is famous for its salubrious climate and vast green meadows.

Visitors can indulge in numerous adventure sports or simply enjoy the tranquility of this "kept-away" destination.

How to Reach

By Air

Gaggal Airport near Kangra is the most convenient. Taxis and buses are available from there to Dalhousie.

By Rail

Pathankot is the nearest railhead, connected to major cities like Delhi via Jammu Mail and Shalimar Express.

By Road

Luxury and standard buses operate from major neighboring cities via Pathankot, Ambala, and Ludhiana.

Heritage Churches

St. Patrick's, St. Francis, and St. John's Churches are other notable colonial landmarks nearby.
