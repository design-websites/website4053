Dharamsala

Queen of the Hills & City of Blue Mountains at the foot of the Dhauladhar Range.

At a Glance

Area: 29 Sq. kms
Altitude: 1380 m above sea level
Best Time: Mar-June, Sept-Oct
Climate: Summer (16°C-26°C), Winter (3°C-6°C)

Overview

Dharamsala is an important township of the Kangra district. Standing at the foot of the snow-wrapped lofty Dhauladhar ranges, it offers magnificent views of snowy peaks, deodar and pine forests, tea gardens, and beautiful hills.

With numerous streams, cool healthy air, and its proximity to the snowline, Dharamsala has everything for a perfect holiday. It is a place that is full of life yet remarkably peaceful.

Prime Attractions

McLeodganj

Originally the home of the semi-nomadic Gaddi tribe, it is now the world-famous residence of His Holiness, the Dalai Lama.

Dal Lake

Surrounded by high green deodar trees, this lake fills a mountain bowl and serves as an enchanting, serene picnic spot approachable by road.

Kangra Art Museum

A treasure trove displaying the valley's arts, crafts, and rich past with artifacts dating back to the 5th century.

Bhagsunath Shrine

An ancient temple featuring sacred fresh water springs close by, highly revered by Hindu devotees.

Spirituality & Heritage

Norbulinka Institute

With heavy Japanese influence, featuring shady paths, wooden bridges, and streams. The nearby nunnery teaches advanced Buddhist philosophy.

Chinmaya Tapovan

A tranquil ashram including a 9m high image of Hanuman, a Ram temple, and a meditation hall.

Masrur Rock Temples

Fifteen richly carved monolithic rock temples sculpted in the splendid style of the Kailash temple at Ellora, dating back to the 8th century.

Nature & Trekking

Triund

Located on the foothills of the Dhauladhar range, offering breathtaking views of mountains and valleys. Ideal for trekking.

Dharmkot

An attractive picnic spot on a hill crest presenting panoramic views of the Kangra valley.

Other notable nature spots include the Tatwani hot springs and the massive waterfall at Machhrial, as well as the cave temples at Trilokpur.

Points of Interest

St. John's Church

Built in 1852 under deodar branches, featuring fine Belgian stained glass windows.

Andretta

A pottery center and gallery housing major works of art.

Kunal Pathri

Rock temples from which the surrounding area derives its name.
