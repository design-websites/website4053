Great Himalayan National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage site preserving the raw beauty of the Western Himalayas.

Park Snapshot

Area: 765 sq. kms
Altitude: 1,300m To 6,100m
Best Time: Apr-June & Sept-Nov
Climate: -5ºC to 30ºC

History & Ecosystem

Located in the upper ranges of the Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh, the Great Himalayan National Park (also known as Jawahar Lal Nehru National Park) was notified in 1984 to protect endangered species.

It represents one of the last undisturbed Western Himalayan ecosystems. A major portion of the park is permanently under glaciers and ice, creating a biological richness that is truly unique to this region of India.

Biodiversity & Wildlife

The Snow Leopard

The highly endangered Snow Leopard is the king of these mountains. While they cannot growl loudly due to weak vocal tissues, they are the park's most elusive attraction.

Avian Wonders

Home to over 203 bird species, including the rare Western Tragopan, the colorful Monal, and the Koklass pheasant.

Mammalian Diversity

Spot exotic species such as the Nilgiri Tahr, Serow, Ghoral, Bharal (Blue Sheep), Brown Bear, Musk Deer, and Red Fox.

Lower Reaches

The Indian Pika and Great Indian Flying Squirrel can be found in the more accessible lower areas of the park.

Trekking & Exploration

Most of the park remains unexplored due to the sheer difficulty of the terrain. This lends the place a unique thrill and rawness. Trekking here requires excellent physical shape and proper equipment, including tents and sleeping bags.

April and May are the best months for trekking as the snow begins to melt.

Note: No horse or mule transport is allowed inside the park boundaries; all exploration must be done on foot.

Travel Guide

By Air

Bhuntar Airport (Kullu) is the nearest, located 10 km from Kullu town. Taxis are readily available.

By Rail

The nearest narrow gauge railhead is at Jogindernagar, approximately 95 km from Kullu.

By Road

Take the eastern divergence at Aut on the Kullu-Manali highway. Aut is 30 km from Kullu.

Important Tips

Consult a doctor before your trip and ensure you have all required permits. Visit the Information Centre at Larjee for park details.
