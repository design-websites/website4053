Dalhousie

A picturesque hill station built across five hills on the Dhauladhar range.

About the Hill Station

Dalhousie is a favorite tourist destination in the Chamba district of Himachal Pradesh. Built across five hills and situated on the Dhauladhar mountain range, it sits between 6,000 and 9,000 feet above sea level.

The town was named after Lord Dalhousie, the British Viceroy who established it as his summer retreat. Today, the town's charm is further enhanced by its beautiful Victorian and Scottish architecture seen in its many bungalows and churches.

Climate & Best Time to Visit

The peak tourist season and best time to visit is from May to September. While Dalhousie maintains a cold climate throughout the year, the weather varies by season:

  • May - July: Warm afternoons, but cold at other times.
  • June - September: Heavy rain and thunder.
  • December - January: Experiences snowfall.

Economy

Tourism is the major industry, contributing approximately 3% to the state's GDP. With roughly 600 hotels, the sector provides employment for 5,000 to 8,000 people annually.

Education

Dalhousie is a hub for boarding schools, attracting students primarily from Punjab and Haryana. Notable institutions include Dalhousie Public School and Hilltop Public School.

Places of Interest

Karelanu

Famous for its natural spring water. It is notably credited with healing Subhash Chandra Bose of tuberculosis during his stay in the region.

Gandhi Chowk

A unique road junction where eight roads meet, connecting major areas like Subhash Chowk, Sadar Bazaar, and Khajjiar.

Scenic Attractions

Top spots include Dainkund Peak, Bakrota Hills, the Aread potato fields near Alla, and the beautiful Khajjiar.
