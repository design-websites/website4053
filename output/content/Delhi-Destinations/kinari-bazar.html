Kinari Bazar

Delhi's vibrant destination for wedding splendor and traditional embellishments.

Shopping Hub

While visiting Delhi, standard shopping destinations typically include Connaught Place, Chandni Chowk, Karol Bagh, Lajpat Nagar, South Extension, and Rani Bagh. However, for those seeking specific traditional treasures, Kinari Bazar is a must-visit.

Why visit Kinari Bazar?

Kinari Bazar is the ultimate destination for wedding and engagement apparel. The market specializes in all types of borders and embellishments, earning its reputation as a centerpiece of traditional craft.

Festival Season

During major festivals such as Dussehra, Diwali, and Karwa Chauth, the bazar becomes a hub for traditional and festival-related items essential for Indian celebrations.

Pro Tip: Bargain!

Bargaining is a standard and expected part of the shopping experience in Kinari Bazar. Don't hesitate to negotiate to get the best value for your traditional finds.
