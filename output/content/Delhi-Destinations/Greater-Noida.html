Greater Noida

An urban industrial marvel and premier commercial hub in the National Capital Region.

Overview

Greater Noida is an urban settlement and commercial area located in the southeastern part of the capital city of New Delhi. As a sub-city of Delhi falling under the National Capital Region (NCR), it is recognized as one of the largest industrial townships in Asia.

The city is a major hub for global IT giants and renowned business houses. It experiences a sub-tropical climate typical of the region and features modern attractions such as Gautam Buddha University, Jaypee Greens golf course, and Knowledge Park.

Premier Venues & Marts

India Expo Centre

Considered India's best event hosting venue, this 21,000 square meter center features six fully air-conditioned halls with world-class facilities and a capacity for 5,000 people.

Exposition Mart

India's first state-of-the-art mart for the Cottage and Handicraft sector. Modeled after the Shanghai Mart, it promotes exports of handlooms, carpets, and jute.

Sports & Future Projects

Formula 1 Track

A world-class 5.5 km racing track designed by Hermann Tilke. Spanning 4,000 hectares with a seating capacity of 200,000, it marks Greater Noida as a premier racing destination.

Night Safari

A planned open zoological garden designed on the lines of established safaris in China and Singapore, aiming to create environmental awareness and spur tourism.

Taj International Airport

A planned international hub to facilitate cargo and tourism, directly connected to the Taj Expressway for seamless access to New Delhi.

Recreation

City Park

A beautifully developed space featuring exotic plants, fountains, and professional landscaping. A unique attraction within the park is the dolphin fish pond, appealing to all age groups.
