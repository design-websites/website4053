Connaught Place (CP)

As the center of Delhi, CP is a bustling mix of heritage and modern commerce. Key highlights include Palika Bazaar and Janpath for street shopping, while the outer circus features top-tier restaurants and branded showrooms.

Chandni Chowk

Located in Old Delhi, this historic bazaar was built by Shah Jahan in 1650. While the rise of modern malls has shifted some trends, it remains the legendary hub for traditional shoppers, specifically for jewelry and marriage essentials. Don't miss Kinari Bazar for specialized event items.

Lajpat Nagar & South Extension

These neighboring residential areas host some of the city's most famous markets. Lajpat Nagar is home to the high-energy Central Market, a favorite for local fashion and ethnic wear.

Exploring the NCR

The National Capital Region (NCR) offers sophisticated shopping experiences in satellite cities:

Gurgaon (Gurugram)

Famous for high-end shopping malls. It is conveniently located on the way to Jaipur from Delhi.

Noida & Faridabad

Vibrant cities with excellent retail options. Both serve as common transit points if traveling from Delhi to Agra.

Rapid development is also transforming Palwal, located on the route to Agra.
