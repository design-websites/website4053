Discover Mehrauli

Step into one of the seven ancient cities of Delhi, where history stands eternal.

Historical Overview

Mehrauli, also known as Mihirawali, is a southern district of Delhi named after its founder, King Mihir Bhoja of the Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty. It is one of the seven ancient cities that constitute Delhi.

Encircled by Hauz Khas, Vasant Kunj, and Tughlakabad, Mehrauli is located close to Gurgaon. The district features architecture designed according to British style by well-known architect AGK Menon.

The Qutub Complex

The most important attraction in Mehrauli is the Qutub Minar. Built by Qutub-ud-din Aibak, the Qutub complex is a classic piece of architecture and a UNESCO World Heritage site visited by tourists year-round.

Qutub Festival

Held annually for three days in November-December, this popular festival organized by Delhi Tourism showcases India's cultural art forms.

The Iron Pillar

Located at the Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque, this 1,600-year-old iron wonder remains rust-free despite exposure to sun, air, and rain. Weighing six tons, the pillar features Brahmi inscriptions suggesting it may belong to Chandragupta II of the Gupta Dynasty.

Originally installed on Vishnupada Hill, it was later brought to its current location near the Qutub Minar by Anangpal Tomar.

Religious Monuments

Khwaja Qutbuddin Bakhtiar Kaki Mosque

A 13th-century mosque that hosts the annual Phoolwalon-ki-sair Festival, a three-day celebration of flower sellers representing brotherhood and friendship.

Other Notable Sites

  • Jamali Kamali Mosque: Built in 1536 for the Sufi saint Shaikh Jamali Kamboh Dihlawi.
  • Moti Masjid: A small mosque built by Bahadur Shah I for private prayer.
  • Yogmaya Temple: An ancient and popular temple located near the Qutub Minar complex.

Historic Tombs & Mysteries

The Bhulbhulaiyan

Constructed in 1566 by Emperor Akbar, Adham Khan’s Tomb is known as Bhulbhulaiyan due to its maze-like passages where one could get lost.

Mehrauli Archeological Park

Home to the 13th-century Indo-Islamic tomb of Slave dynasty ruler Balban and the nearby tomb of his son, Khan Shahid.

Visiting Mehrauli

Location

Southern district of Delhi, accessible from major areas like Hauz Khas and Vasant Kunj.

Festivals

  • Qutub Festival: Nov-Dec
  • Phoolwalon-ki-sair: Annual flower seller's festival

Don't Miss

  • Qutub Minar (UNESCO Site)
  • The Maze (Bhulbhulaiyan)
  • The Mystery Iron Pillar
