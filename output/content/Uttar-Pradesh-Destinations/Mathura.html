Mathura: The Holy Abode

Explore the sacred birthplace of Lord Krishna and the heart of Brij culture.

Krishnajanmabhoomi

Mathura is most reputed as the birthplace of Lord Krishna. Revered by Hindu pilgrims, it is an indispensable stopover on any Uttar Pradesh itinerary. The city has been a hub of culture and civilization for about 3,000 years, held in sanctity by Buddhist, Jain, and Brahmanical faiths alike.

145 Km south-east of Delhi
Best visit: October to March

Major Religious Sites

Shri Krishna Janma Bhoomi

Believed to be the exact site where Lord Krishna was born 5,000 years ago in King Kansa's prison. A stone slab marks the exact spot within the temple.

Dwarkadheesh Temple

Built in 1814 near the Yamuna River, this is the city's most popular temple, known for its splendid architectural traits and elaborate festival decorations.

Vishram Ghat

A sacred spot on the Yamuna where Lord Krishna is said to have rested after defeating the cruel King Kansa.

Krishna Balrama Mandir (ISKCON)

Established in 1975, it is one of the highest standards of Deity worship in Vrindavan, attracting devotees from around the world.

Festivals & Traditions

  • Holi: A boisterous full-moon festival in February or March where people throw color dyes and enjoy processions.

  • Janmashtami: Celebrated with electric atmosphere as thousands gather to celebrate Krishna's birth at midnight with folklore and recitals.

Temples of Vrindavan

Jaipur Temple

Built in 1917 by the Maharaja of Jaipur, featuring opulent workmanship and solid rock pillars.

Radharamana Temple

Home to a deity that self-manifested from a saligram-sila in 1542.

Travel Logistics

By Rail

Important junction; 2-3 hours from Delhi and 1 hour from Agra.

By Road

Located on National Highway 2, 150 km south of Delhi and 50 km north of Agra.

By Air

Nearest airport is Agra (50 KM), but most use IGI Airport New Delhi (150 KM).
