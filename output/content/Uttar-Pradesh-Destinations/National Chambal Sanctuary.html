National Chambal Sanctuary

A Sanctuary for Wildlife and Nature in the Heart of India.

Explore the Sanctuary

The National Chambal Sanctuary is a significant destination for wildlife enthusiasts visiting Uttar Pradesh. It offers a unique opportunity to explore the natural heritage and diverse fauna of Northern India.

As a premier wildlife destination, the sanctuary is part of a broader network of protected areas, providing vital information and experiences for those interested in Indian wildlife conservation.

Nature and Biodiversity

The sanctuary is dedicated to preserving the ecological balance of the region. Visitors can enjoy serene landscapes and witness the raw beauty of the wilderness while learning about the sanctuary's ongoing conservation efforts.

Wildlife Protection

Dedicated to safeguarding rare species and their natural habitats across the Uttar Pradesh landscape.
