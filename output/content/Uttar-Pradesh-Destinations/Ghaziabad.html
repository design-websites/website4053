Ghaziabad: Gateway of UP

The historical intersection of ancient mythology and modern industry.

Discover Ghaziabad

Sharing its boundary with Delhi, Ghaziabad is a bustling hub for industries and an emerging education center within the NCR. The city holds significant mythological and historical roots; Garhmukteshwar is believed to grant salvation, while villages like Pooth are associated with the Mahabharat period.

Key Places of Interest

Hapur

The largest tehsil in the district, Hapur is a major market for grain, jaggery, and papar. It is home to the historic 1670 Jama Masjid built during Aurangzeb's reign.

Modinagar

Founded by Gujar Mal Modi, this industrial town produces essentials like sugar, textiles, and paints. It features a 19th-century temple built by Rani Bala Bai Sindhia.

Bahadurgarh

Renamed by Nawab Bahadur Khan, this town is famous for its Khurja pottery and handloom cloth.

Dasna

Located 10 km from Ghaziabad, its name is linked to a legend of a man being bitten by a snake (Dasna) while building a fort.

Historical & Legend Hubs

  • Ajrara: Situated on the Kali Nadi, named after Yogi Ajaipal who built a thakurdwara here.
  • Dhaulana: Notable for its 1857 freedom struggle memorial and the temple of Sati Malindi Devi.
  • Loni: Associated with the demon 'Lanvanasur' or the legendary King Lonnkaran.

At a Glance

Industrial Hub

Major producer of sugar, soap, varnishes, and flour.

Key Monument

Jama Masjid in Hapur (Built 1670).

NCR Proximity

Directly shares a boundary with the national capital, Delhi.
