Katrnighat Wildlife Sanctuary

A Gateway to the Untamed Terai Wilderness.

Embrace the Wilderness

Katrnighat Wildlife Sanctuary stands as a premier natural haven in Uttar Pradesh. It is a vital component of the region's wildlife ecosystem, offering a sanctuary for numerous species and an escape for those seeking peace amidst nature's grandeur.

This sanctuary is a part of the vibrant wildlife circuit in Northern India, providing an authentic look into the rich biodiversity of the Terai region.

Conservation and Beauty

The sanctuary's landscape is a blend of dense forests and open water bodies, creating a perfect environment for diverse fauna. It is a preferred spot for bird watchers, eco-tourists, and photographers looking to capture the raw beauty of Indian wildlife.

Eco-Tourism Hub

Experience sustainable travel while exploring one of the most significant wildlife reserves in Uttar Pradesh.

Photographer's Paradise

From rare birds to majestic land mammals, every corner offers a new perspective for nature enthusiasts.
