Azamgarh Tour

A land of ancient rishis, philosophical movements, and world-renowned crafts.

Overview of Azamgarh

Azamgarh was founded in 1665 by Azam, the son of Vikramajit. It is famously known as the land of rishi Durvasa. His ashram is located in the Phulpur tehsil, where thousands of his students historically gathered to receive an education.

A Hub of Theology and Poetry

As an important town in Eastern Uttar Pradesh, Azamgarh has been home to the Azamgarh Nationalist Movement initiated by theologian and philosopher Maulana Shibli Nomani. The city is historically significant for its composition of world-famous books on Islam, including the biography of Prophet Muhammad, 'Seerat-ul-Nabi'.

The city is also the home of Kaifi Azami, one of India's most famous poets and intellectuals. Furthermore, it houses the Darul Musannefin Shibli Academy, recognized as the largest Arabic library in Asia.

Places of Interest & Traditional Crafts

Benarsi Sari Udyog, Mubarakpur

This is the district's most important industry, affording maximum employment. It is a village household industry with looms set up in the majority of rural households. The Benarasi saris of Mubarakpur are famous worldwide.

Black Pottery, Nizamabad

As one of the oldest industries in the district, pottery contributes substantially to the local economy. The fancy pottery produced in Nizamabad, including decorative tea-pots, sugar-bowls, and statues of deities like Ganesh and Laxmi, is world-famous.

Regional Economy

While the industrial base is specialized in specific crafts, Azamgarh maintains a good agricultural foundation. Bridging the gap between the agricultural and industrial sectors through a coordinated approach remains key to enriching the district's economy.

How to Reach

By Rail

Connected to Mumbai, Delhi, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Jaipur, Ajmer, and Amritsar.

By Road

Regular buses ply to Lucknow, the state capital, with some services available to New Delhi.

By Air

The closest major hub is Varanasi Airport (75 km away), with another option at Gorakhpur Airport (80 km away).
