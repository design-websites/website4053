Agra Tour

Witness the Symbol of Love and the Grandeur of the Mughal Empire.

The Iconic Taj Mahal

Agra is an essential destination in Northern India, distinguished by the **Taj Mahal**, the most important monument in the country. Built by the Mughal Emperor **Shah Jahan** in the 16th Century for his beloved wife **Mumtaz**, it stands as a timeless symbol of love.

Built with white marbles, gold, and diamonds.
20,000 workers labored for over 10 years.
Fabulous views in both daylight and moonlight.
Considered one of the 7 Wonders of the World.

Located near the beautiful **Yamuna River**, the monument is surrounded by lush gardens, providing a serene backdrop to its magnificent craftsmanship.

The majestic view of Taj Mahal from across the Yamuna River.

Agra Fort (Lal-Quila)

Beyond the Taj Mahal, visitors flock to the **Agra Fort**, also known as the **Lal-Quila of Agra**. This massive red sandstone fortification was built by the Mughal kings and contains historic structures such as:

  • Amar Singh Gate
  • Jahangir Mahal
  • Diwan-a-aam

The Golden Triangle: Agra, Delhi & Jaipur

Agra forms one corner of the famous **Golden Triangle** tourist circuit along with **Delhi** and **Jaipur**.

Discovering Jaipur (The Pink City)

Visit the stunning **Hawa Mahal**, **Jal-Mahal**, and the massive **Amer Fort**. Known as the Pink City, Jaipur's historical buildings reflect the vibrant heritage of Rajasthan.

"Taste the Tradition"

No trip to Agra is complete without tasting **"Petha"**, a local sweet made from pumpkin. Agra's petha is widely considered the best in India.

Travel Logistics

By Rail

Daily direct trains like **Taj Express** and **Rajdhani Express**. Reach Agra in just 3 hours from Delhi.

By Road

Reach in 4-5 hours via the **GT Road** or use the new **Delhi-Noida-Agra Highway** for a faster journey.

Quick Tip

Agra is perfectly doable as a same-day return trip from Delhi.
