Moradabad

The Renowned "Brass City" of Uttar Pradesh

City Profile

Established in 1600 AD by Prince Murad, son of the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan, Moradabad was originally known as Chaupala. Located on the banks of the Ramganga River, it is approximately 167 km from Delhi and has grown into a major industrial center.

Key Stats (2001)

  • Population: 641,240
  • Literacy Rate: 74% (Higher than national average)

Languages

Hindi, Urdu, English, and Punjabi

The Brass Industry

The roots of the Indian Brassware Industry trace back to the 1860s with the migration of Mohammed Yar Khan from Afghanistan. Today, Moradabad is a global powerhouse for brass goods, supporting roughly 5,000 industrial units and 600 export units.

Global Reach

Exports reach the UK, USA, Germany, Canada, and the Middle East, generating over ₹2,200 crore annually.

Beyond brass, the city is a significant exporter of Mentha, contributing an additional ₹400+ crore to the international market every year.

Travel & Logistics

By Road

Strong connectivity to Delhi, Bareilly, Agra, Ghaziabad, and Lucknow.

By Rail

Well-linked to major Indian cities through a robust railway network.

Nearest Airports

  • Jolly Grant (Dehradun) - 162 km
  • IGI Airport (Delhi) - 167 km
