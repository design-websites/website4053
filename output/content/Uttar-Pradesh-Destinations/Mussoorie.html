Mussoorie: Queen of Hills

A charming escape at 2003 meters in the Garhwal hills.

Historical Legacy

Mussoorie, situated 34 kms from Dehradun, is one of the most frequently visited hill stations in India. It was discovered in 1820 by Captain Young of the British army, who was so influenced by its beauty that he made it his residence.

The name is derived from the 'Mussoorie' plants once found here in abundance. Today, it serves as a gateway to major Hindu pilgrimage sites like Gangotri, Yamunotri, Haridwar, and Rishikesh.

Major Sightseeing Highlights

Gun Hill

The second highest peak in Mussoorie. Reachable by a 400m ropeway ride, it offers panoramic views of Himalayan peaks like Bunderpunch and Gangotri.

Kempty Fall

Located 15 kms away on Yamunotri Road. It is the biggest waterfall in the area and a popular spot for refreshing baths.

Municipal Garden

A scenic picnic spot featuring a beautiful garden and an artificial lake with boating facilities.

Mussoorie Lake

A newly developed spot 6 kms from town on the Dehradun road, offering pedal boats and village views.

Historic Sites & Temples

  • Sir George Everest House: Home of the first Surveyor General of India; Mount Everest is named in his honor.
  • Jwalaji Temple: Situated atop Benog Hill (2104m) amidst thick forests, it houses an idol of Mata Durga.
  • Nag Devta Temple: Located 6 kms from town, offering charming views of Doon Valley.
  • Clouds End: A historic bungalow built in 1838, now a resort surrounded by snow-clad Himalayan views.

Plan Your Trip

Area: 65 sq km

Altitude: 2005.5 m

Best Time: April-June and September-October

Climate: Summers max 31.2°C; Winters min 1.1°C

How to Reach

By Air

Jolly Grant Airport (near Dehradun) is 24 km away.

By Rail

Dehradun is the nearest railhead, connected to Delhi, Mumbai, and Lucknow.

By Road

Direct buses from major neighboring cities to Stands like Library or Masonic.
