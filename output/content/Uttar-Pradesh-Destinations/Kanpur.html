Kanpur Tour

Discover the Manchester of India and the largest city in Uttar Pradesh.

Manchester of India

Kanpur is a premier industrial town in India, founded by the Hindu Chandel King of Sachendi. Nestled on the banks of the river Ganga, the city holds significant historical, religious, and commercial importance. As the largest city in Uttar Pradesh, it serves as a massive hub for education, art, and literature.

Major Leather & Textile Center
Home to reputed IIT Kanpur

Relevance of the Name

The city derived its name from Kanhiyapur, the town of Kanhiya (Lord Krishna). Over time, Kanhiyapur was distorted to Kanhapur and eventually became known as Kanpur. The city is also famously associated with Karna, the popular character from the Mahabharat.

Iconic Places to Visit

Green Park Stadium

A gift to cricket lovers, this floodlit stadium has a seating capacity of 45,000 and is the only multi-purpose stadium in Uttar Pradesh for both Test and One Day matches.

Jajmau (Siddhapuri)

This ancient city destination features the Siddhnath and Siddha Devi temples, along with the tomb of sufi saint Makhdam Shah Ala-ul-Haq built by Firoz Shah Tughlaq.

Massacre Ghat

Formerly Satti Chaura Ghat, it gained significance during the 1857 revolt. It was renamed Nana Rao Ghat after Nana Sahib led the revolt and later became popular as Massacre Ghat.

Allen Forest Zoo

Established in 1971, this zoo-cum-botanical garden houses a wide variety of native and foreign animals and plants.

Parks, Gardens & Worship

  • Phool Bagh: Also known as Ganesh Udyan, it hosts annual flower shows and public meetings at the K.E.M. Hall.

  • Moti Jheel: A popular picnic spot featuring beautiful recreation grounds and a children's park.

  • Spiritual Landmarks: Visit the Shri Radhakrishna Temple (J.K. Temple), the Jain Glass Temple, and the historic Bhitargaon Temple.

Traveler Insights

Shopping

Kanpur is a world-renowned destination for high-quality leather and textile goods.

Kamla Tower

Built on the outlines of the London Tower and Big Ben, it serves as the JK Singhania Business family headquarters.

Education Hub

Kanpur houses IIT Kanpur, Harcourt Butler Technological Institute (HBTI), and GSVM Medical College.
