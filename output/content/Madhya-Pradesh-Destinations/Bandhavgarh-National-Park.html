Bandhavgarh National Park

Land of the Tigers and Ancient Legends

Quick Facts

Area: 450 sq.km
Altitude: 800m above sea level
Best Time: February to June
Language: Bagheli, Hindi
Temp Range: 2°C to 42°C
STD Code: 07653

Overview

Situated 197 km northeast of Jabalpur in Madhya Pradesh, Bandhavgarh National Park belongs to the Vindhyan mountain ranges. It is world-renowned for having the highest density of tiger population in the country, with approximately 46 to 52 tigers spotted here.

The park is encircled by wooded mountains and cliffs, featuring wetlands where Kingfishers and Egrets thrive, while vultures nest in the sheer cliffs above.

Mythology & History

The park's name comes from an ancient fort located in its core area, dating back to the mythological era of the Ramayana. Legend says that the architects of the bridge to Lanka built this fort for Lord Rama, who later gifted it to his brother Lakshmana.

In historical times, the state of Rewa was founded here in 1234. The fort came into the Baghela family as dowry and served as the private game preserve (Shikargah) for the Maharajas of Rewa.

Wildlife & Avifauna

Mammals

  • Carnivores: Tiger, Leopard, Sloth Bear, Gray Mongoose
  • Primates: Common Langur, Rhesus Macaque
  • Herbivores: Spotted Deer, Sambar, Gaur, Nilgai

Birds

With over 250 species, it is a bird watcher's paradise.

  • Grey Malabar Hornbill
  • Paradise Flycatcher
  • Steppe Eagle
  • Blossom Headed Parakeet

Places to See within the Park

Shesh Shaiya: 10th-century statue of Lord Vishnu reclining on a serpent.
Chakradhara: A sprawling meadow rich in flora and fauna.
Bari Gufa: The biggest man-made cave dating back to the 10th century.
Climber's Point: Spectacular views of woody climbers amidst giant Sal trees.
Sehra: The biggest meadow, offering great fort views.
Mahaman Pond: Ideal water hole to watch herbivores and carnivores.

Travel Guide

By Air

Fly to Khajuraho (nearest airport) and drive 7-8 hours to the Tala park entrance.

By Rail

Umaria is the nearest station (40 mins drive). Katni is another major junction nearby.

By Road

Connected to Jabalpur (3-4 hrs), Satna (3.5 hrs), and Kanha (7 hrs) via state and private buses.

Nearby Attractions

  • Baghel Museum (Precious belongings of the Maharaja)
  • Village Tala (Rural Indian life)
  • Bhamera Dam (Water bird watching)
  • Chenchpur Waterfall (44 km away)
