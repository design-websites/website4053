Kanha National Park

Step into the heart of the wild in one of India's finest tiger reserves.

Park Overview

Located in the Banjar and Halon Valleys of the Mandla and Balaghat districts, Kanha National Park is a premier destination in Madhya Pradesh. It is world-renowned for its significant population of Royal Bengal Tigers, leopards, sloth bears, and the rare Barasingha.

Area: 1,945 sq.km
Altitude: 600-900 meters
Best Time: April-June & Nov-Jan
Climate: 2°C to 43°C

History & Evolution

The name Kanha is derived either from Kanhar, the local term for clayey soil, or from Kanva, a holy man who lived in the forest. The park was officially created on June 1, 1953, evolving from the earlier Banjar Valley and Hallon Valley sanctuaries.

Originally declared a reserve forest in 1879, the boundaries were expanded in phases, incorporating the Mukki Valley in 1970 and the Hallon Valley in 1974.

Must-Visit Spots

Bamni Dadar

Commonly known as the Sunset Point, this area offers a breathtaking view of the setting sun. It is a prime location to spot sambar, barking deer, and the four-horned antelope.

Kanha Museum

Located inside the park, the museum provides essential insights into the topography and diverse biological aspects of the reserve.

Biodiversity

Mammals

The park hosts about 22 species of mammals. Key sightings include:

  • Tiger
  • Gaur
  • Sloth Bear
  • Swamp Deer
  • Hanuman Langur
  • Blackbuck

Avifauna

With 175–200 recorded species of birds, it is a haven for birders. Look for the Purple Heron, Shikra, and White-eyed Buzzard.

Explore Nearby

Kawardha Palace

A stunning monument built between 1936-39 using Italian marble, set in 11 acres of lush gardens.

Nagpur (Orange City)

Located 260 km away, this historic city is famous for its citrus fruit and serves as a major transportation hub.

Travel Guide

By Air

Nagpur (266 km) is the nearest airport, connected with domestic flights from Mumbai.

By Rail

Jabalpur (169 km) serves as the most convenient railhead.

By Road

Well-connected to Jabalpur (175 km), Raipur (219 km), and Nagpur (266 km).

Internal Distances

  • Kanha-Mukki: 32 km
  • Kanha-Katia: 4 km
