Ranchi: The Waterfall City

A cool, calm industrial hub blessed with verdant landscapes and natural resources.

City Overview

Ranchi is the capital of the young state of Jharkhand and is fast emerging as a significant industrial town. Known as the "Manchester of the East," the city is rich in natural resources, contributing 50% of the state's total mineral production. It is famous as the home of cricket legend Mahendra Singh Dhoni and served as the historic center for the Jharkhand statehood movement.

Climate & Best Time to Visit

Encircles by hills and dense tropical forests, Ranchi enjoys a pleasing climate throughout the year, which is why it once served as the summer capital of Bihar.

Summers: 20°C to 42°C
Winters: 5°C to 25°C

Best Time: September to May

The City of Waterfalls

Hundru Falls

A fascinating spot where the Subarnarekha River falls from a height of 320 feet. It is one of the most popular picnic destinations in the region.

Jonha Falls

Also known as Gautamdhara, this stepped waterfall requires a 500-step descent. Nearby attractions include a Buddha temple and the Kanchi River.

Dassam Falls

Formed by the Kanchi River cascading from 144 feet near Taimara village. The area is perfect for river rafting and swimming.

Panch Gagh & Hirni

Other beautiful gems include the Panch Gagh falls and the scenic Hirni falls, known for their verdant surroundings.

Major Landmarks

Rock Garden

The most visited spot in the city, featuring fabulous granite sculptures, waterfalls, and swings set in natural surroundings.

Sun Temple

Located 40 kms from Ranchi, this architectural marvel is sculpted like an 18-wheeled chariot pulled by seven white horses.

Ranchi Hill & Pahari Mandir

A major landmark of the town. The hilltop Pahari Mandir is dedicated to Lord Shiva and offers panoramic views.

Tagore Hill

Famous for its association with Rabindranath Tagore, who is believed to have written many of his works at this serene location.

Visitor Info

Economic Hub

A fast-emerging industrial town and a hub for business travelers due to mineral wealth.

Education

Home to several reputed educational institutions.

Nearby Spots

  • Gonda Hill & Kanke Dam
  • Jagannathpur Hill (Kalinga style temple)
  • Kanchi River Water Sports
