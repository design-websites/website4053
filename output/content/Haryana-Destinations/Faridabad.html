Faridabad: The Industrial Hub

A historic township founded in 1607, blending commercial might with cultural heritage.

Historical Legacy

Founded in 1607 A.D. by Shaikh Farid, the treasurer for Emperor Jahangir, Faridabad was originally designed to protect the Delhi-Mathura Highway. The city is uniquely planned in a semi-circle, with its vast industrial zones positioned on the outskirts.

  • Founder: Shaikh Farid (Baba Farid)
  • Region: National Capital Region (NCR)
  • Economy: Major Industrial Center
  • Boundaries: Delhi (North), UP (East), Gurgaon (West)

Major Attractions

Suraj Kund Tourist Complex

An idyllic picnic spot built by Rajput King Surajpal Tomar. It features a historic lake with rock-cut steps and the 'Rajhans' Siddha Kund, a fresh water pool believed to have therapeutic properties.

Surajkund Crafts Mela

Held annually in February, this world-famous fair brings together skilled artisans from across India. It is a vibrant celebration of traditional handicrafts, handlooms, and folk culture.

Nahar Singh Stadium

A major venue for One Day International (ODI) cricket matches with a capacity of 25,000. It houses a state-of-the-art sports complex for swimming and football.

Baba Farid's Tomb

A popular pilgrimage center dedicated to the Sufi saint after whom the city is named. Locals believe the saint founded the city to safeguard the region.

Industry & Shopping

Industrial Prowess

Faridabad is a leader in Heena production and manufacturing of tractors, motorcycles (Escorts), switch gears, refrigerators, and tyres.

Modern Retail

The city boasts a vibrant mall culture with centers like Crown Plaza, SRS Mall, and Crown Interiorz offering premium shopping experiences.

Quick Facts

Annual Crafts Fair

Every February at Suraj Kund.

Lakes

Badhkal Lake (Aravalli Range) and the picturesque Peacock Lake.

Leisure

Aravali Golf Club and Siddhadata Temple complex.
