Chandigarh: The Fort of Chandi

India's first planned city, renowned for its urban design and architectural brilliance.

Historical Planning

Following the 1947 partition, a new capital was needed for Punjab as Lahore became part of Pakistan. Commissioned by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru, the city was designed by the French architect Le Corbusier. Completed in 1961, Chandigarh serves as the union territory and capital for both Punjab and Haryana.

First Planned City of India
Highest Per Capita Income in India
Cleanest City in India (Urban Dev. Ministry)
Name Origin: Fort of Chandi

Must-Visit Destinations

Rock Garden & Sukhna Lake

Iconic landmarks of the city. The Rock Garden showcases unique sculptures made from industrial and home waste, while Sukhna Lake offers a peaceful retreat at the foothills of the Shivaliks.

Rose Garden

Part of the Leisure Valley, this is the largest garden in Asia. It houses thousands of rose varieties and is a central part of the city's vast "Leisure Valley" park system.

Capitol Complex

The administrative hub designed by Le Corbusier, featuring the Open Hand Monument, the Legislative Assembly, and the High Court.

A City of Gardens

Known for its greenery, the city features the Botanical Garden, Bougainvillea Garden, Garden of Fragrance (Aromatic Plants), and the Garden of Rare Plants.

Cosmopolitan Culture

Chandigarh's culture is a vibrant mix influenced by neighboring states like Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, UP, and Bihar.

  • Diverse North Indian cuisine.
  • Modern dressing with traditional influences (Salwar suits, Trousers, and Jeans).
  • Home to the ancient Chandi temple.

How to Reach

By Air

The domestic airport connects Chandigarh to almost every major city in India.

By Rail

Excellent connectivity via Shatabdi, Jan Shatabdi, and Himalayan Queen from Delhi.

By Road

250 km from Delhi via NH 21 and NH 22. Taxis and buses are readily available.

Shopping Hub

Sector 17 is the city's primary shopping and commercial center.
