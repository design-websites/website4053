Gurgaon: Commercial Hub

From a quiet suburb to India's fastest-growing business and IT destination.

Location and History

Once a quiet suburb of Delhi, Gurgaon has transformed into the fastest developing town in India and a major IT hub. Mythologically, it is said the city was gifted by the Kauravas and Pandavas to their Guru as homage. Today, it stands as a leading commercial and residential center on the outskirts of Delhi.

Fastest Growing IT & Business Field

Unofficial Mall Capital of India

Key Tourist Spots

Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary

Located 15km from the city, this sanctuary founded by Dr. Salim Ali is home to about 100 species of birds. Winter is the best time to visit the watch towers and museum for migratory bird sightings.

Damdama Lake

A pretty and eye-catching spot for nature lovers. The Water Banks resort provides luxury comforts and sports like hot air ballooning and boating.

Sheetala Devi Temple

A popular Hindu temple known as a 'Shakti Peeth'. It attracts a large number of devotees throughout the year seeking spiritual blessings.

Bhondsi Forest Reserve

A spectacular destination for flora and fauna lovers. It features a spectacular white statue of Lord Shiva standing atop a mountain.

Lifestyle and Entertainment

Gurgaon is the ultimate destination for those who want to experience modern urban India. Known for its Hi-Tech Malls, multiplexes, and brand outlets, it quenches the thirst of every shopping enthusiast.

Shopping: Unofficial Mall Capital with global brand outlets.
Youth Hub: Booming with discos, pizza corners, and vibrant nightlife.

Nearby Excursions

Nuh

A town of historical significance featuring shaking towers and Sheikh Musa’s tomb.

Sohna

Famed for its red sandstone masonry tank and architecture blending Muslim and Rajput styles.

Bhondsi Village

Mesmerizing views during dusk, ideal for those seeking scenic forest beauty.
