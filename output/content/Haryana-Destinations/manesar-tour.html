Manesar: The New Gurgaon

A fast-growing industrial town nestled in the scenic Aravalli ranges.

History and Location

Manesar is a rapidly developing industrial town located in the Gurgaon district of Haryana. Situated in the lower Aravalli ranges on slightly elevated ground, it has transformed from a sleepy town of 1,000 dwellings into a booming industrial hub where over 10,000 people work daily.

Places to Visit

Manesar Village

Located on the outskirts, the village offers the charm of a traditional Rajasthani Haveli. It features sophisticated rooms and authentic Rajasthani cuisine, perfect for holiday lovers.

Modern Living

The town is unique for its self-contained residential complexes that function like mini-cities, equipped with gyms, swimming pools, health clubs, and shopping centers.

Pataudi Palace

A major attraction of historical importance located near Manesar, showcasing the heritage of the Nawabs of Pataudi.

Heritage Architecture

Explore Farukh Nagar for its red stone Mughal mosque or visit the Tomb of Sheikh Musa in Nuh, which blends Muslim and Rajput architecture.

Nearby Nature & Excursions

  • Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary: Located just a few kilometers away, it is home to numerous rare and migratory water birds.

  • Damdama Lake: A popular spot near Manesar offering boat rides, hot air ballooning, and camel rides.

  • Sohna Sulphur Springs: Famous for hot water springs with medicinal properties, located in close proximity to the town.
