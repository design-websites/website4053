Rohtak: The Educational Hub

A vital satellite city of New Delhi and a cornerstone of Haryana's academic and industrial growth.

Historical Legacy

Rohtak, whose name is derived from Rohtashgarh or Raja Rohtash, is a significant municipality in Haryana. Located 70 km west of New Delhi and 210 km south of Chandigarh, it serves as a prominent satellite city and part of the National Capital Region.

Total Area: 1745 sq. km
Population: 940,128 (2001 Census)
Premier Academic Destination
Major Industrial Hub

Academic Excellence

Top Universities

  • Indian Institute of Management (IIM): A top-ranked Indian institute.
  • Maharishi Dayanand University: A cornerstone of higher education.
  • Engineering Institutes: Vaish College and Matu Ram Institute.
  • Sanskriti College of Education: Premier teacher training center.

Notable Schools

  • Model School: Renowned for academic standards.
  • D.A.V. Centenary Public School: A leading public school.
  • The Sanskriti School: Modern holistic education.
  • Shiksha Bharti Sr. Sec. School: Quality secondary education.

Industrial Powerhouse

Haryana state has urbanized a massive industrial township in Rohtak covering 3,000 acres. This hub has attracted global and national leaders, fostering significant economic development in the region.

Economy & Sports

Agricultural Economy

Approximately 51.89% of the workforce is engaged in farming. The region is progressive, utilizing modern machinery like versatile threshers, seed tools, and mechanical weeding wheels.

Sports Infrastructure

Rohtak features a massive sports complex including a Cricket Pavilion, Soccer Stadium, Hockey Ground, and specialized halls for Boxing and Wrestling.
