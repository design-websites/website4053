Bhiwani: Small Kashi

A spiritual sanctuary and the powerhouse of Indian boxing nestled in Haryana.

Historical Legacy

Created on December 22, 1972, Bhiwani was named by Rajput King Neem after his wife, Bhani. Known as the "City of Temples" and "Small Kashi" of India, it boasts over 300 temples and has been a prominent commerce center since the Mughal era.

300+ Temples across the city

Cradle of Freedom Fighters

Major Attractions

Spiritual Landmarks

  • Sri Hari Har Temple: A revered city landmark.
  • Devsar Dhaam: Home to the Maa Durga Temple.
  • Jain Temple: Located in Ranila Village.
  • Baba Jyotram Temple: Situated in Badala Village.

Boxing Powerhouse

Established by legendary boxer Late Capt. Hawa Singh, the Bhiwani Boxing Club has emerged as the primary center for Indian boxing, earning the title of India's boxing powerhouse.

Freedom Heritage

The city produced numerous freedom fighters, including Neki Sharma (honored as Haryana Kesri), who played a pivotal role in the 1922 Bardoli Satyagraha.

Education Hub

Home to the Technological Institute of Textile and Sciences by Birla Group, the Haryana Shiksha Board, and multiple engineering and degree colleges.

Regional Geography

Travel Guide

By Rail

Bhiwani Junction connects to Delhi, Jaipur, Alwar, Rohtak, and Bhatinda via three major lines.

By Road

Well-connected by Haryana State Transport to all major towns in the region.

By Air

The most convenient flight connectivity is via Indira Gandhi International Airport in Delhi.
