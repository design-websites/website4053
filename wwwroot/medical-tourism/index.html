Medical Tourism in India


India has emerged as a popular destination for medical tourism in recent years due to its high-quality medical facilities, skilled healthcare professionals, and affordable treatment costs. Many people from around the world travel to India to receive medical treatment, including complex surgeries, specialized procedures, and alternative therapies.

India has a large number of hospitals, clinics, and specialized treatment centers that are equipped with state-of-the-art medical equipment and technology. These facilities offer a wide range of medical services, including cardiology, orthopedics, neurology, oncology, and cosmetic surgery, among others.

In addition, India is known for its skilled healthcare professionals, including doctors, surgeons, and nurses, who are trained to provide the highest standards of care. Many healthcare professionals in India have received training and education from leading institutions around the world, and are renowned for their expertise and skill.

Another advantage of seeking medical treatment in India is the low cost of treatment compared to other countries. Even with the cost of travel and accommodation factored in, medical treatment in India can often be significantly cheaper than in other countries.

Overall, India's advanced medical facilities, skilled healthcare professionals, and affordable treatment costs have made it an attractive destination for people seeking high-quality medical care. This has helped to boost India's economy and position it as a leading destination for medical tourism in the world.

Main hospitals for medical tourism

India has several hospitals that are well-known for medical tourism due to their high-quality medical facilities, skilled healthcare professionals, and affordable treatment costs. Some of the famous hospitals for medical tourism in India include:

Apollo Hospitals - Apollo Hospitals is a chain of hospitals located in various parts of India, and is known for its world-class medical facilities and expertise in several specialties including cardiology, orthopedics, neurology, and oncology.

Fortis Healthcare - Fortis Healthcare is another popular hospital chain in India that offers a wide range of medical services including cardiac care, neurology, oncology, and orthopedics.

Medanta - The Medicity - Medanta is a multi-specialty hospital located in Gurgaon near New Delhi, and is known for its advanced medical facilities and expertise in several specialties including cardiology, neurology, and oncology.

Tata Memorial Hospital - Tata Memorial Hospital is a renowned cancer treatment and research center located in Mumbai, and is known for its advanced cancer treatments and research programs.

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) - AIIMS is a government-run hospital located in New Delhi that is known for its world-class medical facilities and expertise in several specialties including cardiology, neurology, and oncology.

These are just a few examples of the many hospitals in India that are popular for medical tourism. Overall, India has a large number of hospitals and specialized treatment centers that offer high-quality medical services at affordable prices, making it an attractive destination for medical tourists from around the world.

What are cost advantage of going to India for medical treatment

how much it cost in India for cardiac procedure

The cost of a cardiac procedure in India can vary widely depending on the type of procedure, the hospital or clinic, the location, and the patient's individual needs. However, in general, the cost of cardiac procedures in India is much lower than in other developed countries like the US, UK, or Australia.

For example, the cost of a coronary angioplasty in India can range from around 1 lakh to 2 lakhs Indian rupees (approximately $1,350 to $2,700 USD), while the same procedure in the US can cost upwards of $30,000 USD. Similarly, the cost of a bypass surgery in India can range from 3 lakhs to 5 lakhs Indian rupees (approximately $4,050 to $6,750 USD), while the same procedure in the US can cost upwards of $100,000 USD.

It is important to note that the cost of a cardiac procedure in India can vary widely depending on the hospital or clinic, the location, and the patient's individual needs. It is always recommended to research and compare the costs and quality of different hospitals and clinics before making a decision.

For which diseases people consider treatment in India

what are common diseases for which medical tourism is used in India?

Medical tourism in India is primarily used for the treatment of various diseases and medical conditions, some of which include:

Cardiac procedures: India is known for its expertise in cardiac care, and many people come to India for procedures like angioplasty, bypass surgery, and heart valve replacement.

Orthopedic treatments: India has highly skilled orthopedic surgeons and state-of-the-art facilities for joint replacements, spinal surgeries, and other orthopedic procedures.

Cancer treatments: Many people come to India for cancer treatments, as India has world-class cancer centers like the Tata Memorial Hospital, and offers advanced treatments like chemotherapy, radiation therapy, and immunotherapy.

Fertility treatments: India is a popular destination for people seeking fertility treatments like IVF and surrogacy, as these treatments can be much more affordable than in other countries.

Cosmetic surgeries: India has experienced plastic surgeons who perform a wide range of cosmetic surgeries, including facelifts, tummy tucks, and breast augmentation.

These are just a few examples of the medical conditions for which people come to India for medical treatment. Overall, India has a wide range of medical facilities and highly skilled healthcare professionals who provide high-quality care for many different medical conditions, making it a popular destination for medical tourism.

How do people /patient follow up after treatment

how do people continue follow up after getting medical treatment from India

After getting medical treatment from India, people can continue their follow-up care in a number of ways, depending on their individual needs and preferences. Here are some common options:

Telemedicine: Many hospitals and clinics in India offer telemedicine services, which allow patients to consult with their doctors remotely, using video conferencing or other technology. This can be a convenient way for patients to get follow-up care without having to travel back to India.

Local doctors: Patients can also seek out local doctors in their home country who are familiar with the medical treatment they received in India. These doctors can help with follow-up care, monitor the patient's progress, and provide any additional treatment that may be needed.

Medical tourism companies: Some medical tourism companies offer follow-up care services as part of their packages. This may include arranging for local doctors to provide follow-up care, scheduling any necessary tests or procedures, and helping with any other needs or concerns.

Travel back to India: In some cases, patients may need to travel back to India for follow-up care or additional treatment. This can be a good option for patients who want to see their doctors in person or who require specialized care that is only available in India.

Ultimately, the best way for patients to continue their follow-up care after receiving medical treatment in India will depend on their individual needs and circumstances. It is important to discuss these options with the doctors and healthcare providers involved in their care to determine the best course of action.

Summary of reasons - why consider medical tourism in india

what are common reasons people use medical tourism?

People use medical tourism for various reasons, but some of the common reasons are:

Cost savings: One of the primary reasons people opt for medical tourism is to save money on medical treatments. Medical treatments in countries like India, Thailand, and Mexico can often cost significantly less than in developed countries like the US or UK.

Access to specialized treatments: Some medical treatments may not be available in a person's home country, or may be too expensive. Medical tourism allows people to access specialized treatments that they may not be able to get at home.

Reduced waiting times: In some countries, there may be long waiting times for certain medical treatments. Medical tourism allows people to receive treatment more quickly and efficiently.

High-quality medical care: Many countries that are popular for medical tourism, such as India and Thailand, have state-of-the-art medical facilities and skilled healthcare professionals who provide high-quality medical care.

Travel opportunities: Some people choose to combine medical treatment with a vacation, allowing them to enjoy the sights and culture of the destination country while receiving medical care.

Overall, medical tourism offers people the opportunity to access high-quality medical care at an affordable price, while also providing the chance to explore new destinations and cultures.
