Plan a trip to temples in India


Plan for Trip to Somnath Temple

Activity Details
Mode of Transport Flight to Diu Airport or Train to Veraval Railway Station
Accommodation Book a hotel or resort in Somnath or nearby areas
Places to Visit Somnath Temple, Triveni Sangam, Bhalka Tirth, Junagadh Gate, Panch Pandav Gufa, Kamnath Mahadev Temple
Best Month to Visit October to February
Things to Do Attend the evening aarti at Somnath Temple, enjoy the beach at Somnath, try local Gujarati cuisine, shop for souvenirs

Sabarimala Temple Trip Plan

Month Weather Crowd Best Time to Visit
November - December Cool and Pleasant High During Mandala Pooja and Makaravilakku Festival
January - February Cool and Pleasant Low After Makaravilakku Festival
March - May Hot and Humid Low Avoid during Summer
June - October Rainy Season Low Avoid during Monsoon

To visit Sabarimala Temple with family, it is recommended to plan the trip during the months of November to February when the weather is cool and pleasant. The best time to visit is during the Mandala Pooja and Makaravilakku Festival. However, the crowd during this time is high, so it is advisable to plan the trip well in advance and book the accommodation and travel tickets. After the Makaravilakku Festival, the crowd is low, and the weather is still pleasant, making it a good time to visit. It is advisable to avoid visiting during the summer months of March to May and the monsoon season of June to October.

Plan for Trip to Shirdi Sai Baba Temple

Activity Details
Mode of Transportation Book a flight to Aurangabad Airport or take a train to Shirdi Railway Station
Accommodation Book a hotel near the temple or opt for a stay in the temple premises
Temple Visit Visit the temple early in the morning to avoid crowds and long queues
Other Places to Visit Visit Dwarkamai, Chavadi, and Shani Shingnapur
Best Month to Visit October to March is the best time to visit as the weather is pleasant
